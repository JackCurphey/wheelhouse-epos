## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Takings is till money only
The report's Takings SHALL be till money only and SHALL equal Card + Cash + Other. Shopify orders and deposits paid online SHALL be on the online money line instead (Jack's answer 7, 27 Oct). A Cycle to Work hand-over's provider share SHALL be its own figure, "Cycle to Work · Owed by providers, not in the drawer", beside the takings and not in them, like the online money (Jack, 14 Nov, merge-c2w Q1 option 2). There SHALL be no card machine check until a real card machine is connected (Jack's answer 4).

#### Scenario: A deposit paid online
- **WHEN** a repair was paid partly by a deposit paid online and partly by card at the till
- **THEN** Takings and Card show the card part, and the deposit is on the online money line

#### Scenario: A Cycle to Work hand-over
- **WHEN** a day's one sale took £120.00 by card, £5.00 on a gift card and the rest as a Cycle to Work provider's share
- **THEN** the close's Takings is £125.00, Cycle to Work shows the provider's share, and Other shows £5.00
