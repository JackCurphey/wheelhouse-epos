## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The shop can use its own booking terms
Settings › Workshop › Online booking SHALL show which booking terms customers agree to, offer "Use your own" starting from Wheelhouse's standard terms, save the shop's own words when the box is left, and offer "Go back to Wheelhouse's standard terms" (Jack, 8 Oct, not-drawn pages row 95; 20 Oct).

#### Scenario: Own terms
- **WHEN** the owner presses Use your own, edits the terms and leaves the box
- **THEN** they are saved, and customers booking from then on agree to them

#### Scenario: Back to the standard terms
- **WHEN** the owner presses Go back to Wheelhouse's standard terms
- **THEN** customers agree to the standard terms again
