## Context

Part 1 built Reports on the closes Close the day saves. What exists to build on:

- **Closes.** `day_closes` (migrations 096 and 106) holds one close per shop per day (`UNIQUE (shop_id, day)`): who and when, the float check it used, the cash and its expected figure, counted, difference and reason, float left, banked, `carried_pence`, and the day's takings, card, other and online money, kept as they were at the close so the report never changes. Each sale is marked with the close that counted it (`sales.day_close_id`, `till_sales.day_close_id`, one shop-and-close link), so what a close counted is exactly the rows it marked. A day nobody closed is closed later from the morning count that counted it (`float_checks.for_day`, design of `unclosed-day`). `server/routes/close-the-day.js` is the one place that writes a close.
- **Reports.** `server/routes/reports.js` (Owner only, read-only) reads closes; part 1's Takings and cash-ups and a closed day's report (`closeOn(day)` shared with Close the day) are what gain "Sent to Xero" and Reopen this day here.
- **Adapters.** Outside services sit behind a provider chosen by a setting, with a pretend one: `server/payments/` (`index.js` returns the provider or null; `pretend.js` keeps state in Wheelhouse's tables; `provider.js` reads the setting), `server/signin/`.
- **Your data.** `src/screens/settings/your-data-settings.tsx` and `server/shop-data-copy.js` (Download everything: one file per list, plain numbers, `server/lib/csv.js`), built in `your-data-download` and merged (#667, #668, #678).
- **Today.** `server/routes/today.js` builds Needs attention from kinds; "A day nobody closed" (`day_not_closed`) already links to Close the day for that day (`/workshop/till/close?day=…`).
- **Not recorded yet.** No refund of a sale is recorded with its money, no discount keeps its amount and reason (WP-3.1), no sale line keeps a category or a cost at the moment of sale, the old sales record keeps no VAT (`083_all_sales_view.sql`; part 2's answer 3), and Cycle to Work is WP-7.1. The summary can only carry what is recorded.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:** reopen a closed day with a reason and close it again, with the old close kept; connect (pretend) accounts software, choose each line's account once, send each closed day as one summary, correct a reopened day's summary, say what was sent and what couldn't go; and offer the same daily figures as a download.

**Non-Goals:** a real Xero or QuickBooks account or its OAuth; Cycle to Work lines (WP-7.1); several shops (WP-5.2); managers (WP-1.1); the VAT report and the other reports (parts 2 and 3); recording refunds, discounts or costs (WP-3.1, part 2's PR 5); a notification when a send fails beyond Today's line.

## Decisions

1. **Reopen keeps the close as history and re-closes the same row.** `day_closes` gains `reopened_at`, `reopened_by` (the login) and `reopen_reason`, null while closed. A reopened close **doesn't count as closed**: every report reads closes where `reopened_at IS NULL`, so the day's figures drop out of the totals and the report says so (its own row keeps the figures it was closed with and opens its report, Jack, 12 Nov); the day goes back to Today's Needs attention. The first close's figures are copied to a new table `day_close_history` (one row per close that was reopened: the saved figures, who closed it and when, who reopened it and why) at the moment of reopening, so nothing is lost. Closing again **updates the same row** (the unique day stays unique) with the new count, clears the reopen fields and keeps the history. Chosen over deleting the close (the history and what was sent would be lost) and over a second row per day (every report and `UNIQUE (shop_id, day)` assume one). *Jack's answer 1 (12 Nov): a sale saved after the first close is left for the next close.*
2. **The sales stay marked with the reopened day's close.** Reopening un-marks nothing, so what the day counted is exactly what it counted. Closing again works its figures out from the sales that close marked, as the first close did, with the new cash count and banking. A sale made after the first close was never marked, so it belongs to the next close (the close-the-day rule, "a sale made after a close counts in the next day's takings") (Jack's answer 1, 12 Nov).
3. **Only the latest closed day can be reopened** (Jack's answer 4, 12 Nov). The next day's expected cash and carried amounts start from what the earlier close banked and left, so reopening an earlier day while a later one is closed would change what the later close was worked out from. The drawing reopens Wednesday while Thursday is still open, which fits.
4. **The accounts adapter is a provider like payments.** `server/accounts/index.js` returns the connected provider or null; `pretend.js` is the only one; `provider.js` reads the shop's connection. The calls a real Xero or QuickBooks must answer: `connect(shop, who)` (returns a connection), `listAccounts(connection)` (the accounts the shop could choose, for the choices), `postSummary(connection, summary)` (returns an id), `replaceSummary(connection, id, summary)` (a corrected day), `disconnect(connection)`. The pretend one keeps each posted summary in `accounts_pretend_summaries` and offers a small fixed list of accounts named as the drawing's placeholder "[Xero account]". It can be told to refuse for tests (a lost connection, a failing send). Connecting a real service later is a new provider file and Jack's yes.
5. **Three small tables, one migration each, per pull request.** (a) `day_closes` reopen columns and `day_close_history` (PR 1). (b) `accounts_connections` (one row per shop: provider `xero` or `quickbooks`, connected by, when, status `connected` or `lost`, disconnected when) and `accounts_choices` (one row per line: the line's key, such as `sales:Workshop` or `money:Cash`, and the account chosen), kept when disconnected "in case you reconnect" (PR 2). (c) `accounts_sends` (one row per shop per day: status `sent`, `waiting` or `failed`, the reason, when, the amount, the summary as sent, the provider's id, and a `version` that goes up when a reopened day is sent again) and the pretend summaries table (PR 3). Each is additive with row-level security as every shop table.
6. **The summary is built from the close's own sales, and kept as sent.** One summary per shop per closed day: **Sales go to**: the day's sales by category before VAT, each to the account chosen for it; **VAT on sales**: the day's VAT to the one VAT account; **Money taken goes to**: the day's money by how it was paid (Cash, Card, Customer accounts, Gift cards; store credit with Gift cards, as the drawing's Other tile words "Gift cards, store credit and customer accounts"); **Everything else goes to**: Cash differences (the close's difference), Refunds and Discounts given. It is worked out from the rows the close marked (so it can't differ from the report) and saved in `accounts_sends` so what was sent is kept even if rules change. Where Wheelhouse doesn't record a line yet (refunds, discounts given, VAT for sales in the old record, a category at the time of sale), the summary says so rather than guessing; Jack's answer 2 (12 Nov): send each day with what is recorded and show each unrecorded line as "not recorded" in that day's log entry. The summary must balance: money taken equals sales plus VAT plus cash differences, refunds and discounts; a day that doesn't balance is not sent and shows as "Not sent" with the reason (the audit's M15: "a day's entry may not balance").
7. **When a day is sent.** Straight after a close is saved (or a reopened day's new close), if the shop is connected and every line it uses has an account: a send that fails or finds a category with no account chosen leaves the day `failed` (with the reason, shown as the drawing's "[Category] has no Xero account chosen"); when disconnected or lost the day is `waiting` ("sends when Xero is reconnected"). Choosing the missing account, or reconnecting, sends the days that were waiting or failed, oldest first, one at a time. A reopened day's earlier summary is **replaced**, not added to (`replaceSummary`), so the accounts software never holds both; between reopening and closing again the day shows "Reopened" and nothing is sent.
8. **"Sent to Xero" shows only while connected or lost.** Without accounts software the column isn't drawn (Reports reads the connection). Its badges, as drawn: Sent (green), Not closed yet (grey), Reopened (amber), Not sent (amber); a day waiting shows the drawing's Waiting (grey).
9. **Owner only.** Connecting, choosing accounts, disconnecting and reopening are the Owner's (`ownerOnly`), as Settings are, until roles (WP-1.1), which replaces the check in one place. The Accounts software fold is shown to the Owner only; routes are `/api/accounts/*` and `/api/reports/days/:id/reopen`, each carrying `// screens: rp-…`.
10. **The daily figures download.** "Download everything" gains one list, "Daily figures": one row per closed day, in the order of the log, with the same lines the summary carries (the day, takings by how paid, sales by category, VAT, the cash difference, and whether it was sent), plain numbers like every other list. It is available with or without accounts software ("Not using either? 'Download everything' above has the same daily figures to import"), and reads the same stored summary where there is one and builds it from the close where there isn't.
11. **Today's two lines.** A day that couldn't go: "[Wednesday 16 September] didn't go to [Xero]" with "[Bolton] · closed at [time] · [Category] has no Xero account chosen" and "Choose an account for [Category]" (`rp-today-accounts`, `opening.mjs:151`; the audit's H5 fixed the example to a closed day, not today). A reopened day: the existing "wasn't closed … Close it" line with "was reopened" wording; the drawing doesn't draw it, so this follows the closest built pattern and is logged.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [Reopening is a change to a saved close, in the money area] → the first close is copied to history before anything changes, only the latest closed day can be reopened, and the test for it compares every figure before and after; `/review` and the risky-area reviewer.
- [A summary that doesn't balance posts a wrong entry to a shop's books] → the pretend service refuses an unbalanced summary like a real one would, the builder checks balance before sending, and the audit's M15 note stays on the screen: "Check these choices with your accountant before the first day is sent."
- [VAT is exact only for sales in the new record] → the day says so (Jack's answer 2); nothing works VAT out from today's product rates.
- [The accounts adapter is built against a pretend service and the real ones differ] → the adapter's calls are the smallest set (post, replace, list accounts, disconnect); a real provider may need more, which is a later change.
- [Choosing an account later sends days that waited] → one at a time, oldest first, each recorded, and a day already sent is never sent twice (a `version` and the provider's id).

## Migration Plan

Three migrations, additive and nullable or new tables, one per pull request that needs one (PR 1: reopen and history; PR 2: connection and choices; PR 3: what was sent and the pretend summaries); each tested from empty on a scratch database and run twice. Rolling back a pull request is a revert; the tables are left (empty or unused) as migrations always are.

## Jack's answers

Answered on 12 Nov 2026 (`docs/decisions/2026-11-12-reports-part-4-answers.md`), each as recommended and already written into the decisions above and the specs:

1. **What a re-close counts after a day is reopened:** the day's own sales only; a sale saved after the first close stays for the next close.
2. **What goes in the summary that Wheelhouse doesn't record yet:** send each day with what is recorded, and show each unrecorded line as "not recorded" in that day's log entry, for the accountant to fill in.
3. **Online sales on the choices:** an "Online sales (paid on the website)" row in Money taken goes to.
4. **Which closed days can be reopened:** only the latest closed day.
