### Requirement: The booking page shows the conversation with the shop
While the bike is in the shop, the customer's booking page SHALL show "Notes with the shop": the notes so far, a box to add a note (then to reply), and that the shop will reply there and text them (ac-job-note).

- **WHEN** Maya sends "Can you check the back light too?"
