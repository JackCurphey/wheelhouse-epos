# UX walk-through 6 — a repair at a Lightspeed shop

Walked 2 Oct 2026 by the walk-through helper, as story 6 of `docs/decisions/2026-10-02-ux-walkthrough.md` (decision 6: walk every remaining story). This is not a UI audit; journey 21 had its own (`lightspeed-ui-audit.md`), and decision 10 of `2026-10-02-lightspeed-shops-review.md` took every recommendation from it. This walk is walk-through 1's repair, told again at a shop that keeps Lightspeed as its till. It follows Maya's bike from booking to hand-over, as the people living it, and looks for what breaks where Wheelhouse hands over to Lightspeed and back.

**The story.** Maya Patel books a Standard service for her Trek Domane AL 3 at North Street Cycles, Bolton, drops it off, and approves a quote on her phone: WH-1042, £111.00 agreed (journeys 3 and 4, as a Lightspeed shop's customer sees them). Approval makes a Lightspeed work order (decision 3), linking Maya to a Lightspeed customer (decision 4). Alex Morgan (Mechanic) does the work on a tablet and presses "Mark ready for collection". Maya gets "Bike ready" and comes in. Jo Taylor (Staff) takes the payment at the Lightspeed till. Wheelhouse sees the work order paid (decision 2), and Jo presses "Hand over". Jack Lewis (Owner) connected Lightspeed earlier and watches Today. Walked as Maya (phone), Jo (desktop), Alex (tablet) and Jack (desktop), and again as someone using a screen reader or only a keyboard, and as someone with low vision.

**How facts are handled.** Every finding names the screens (id and size) and the source line or decision it rests on. Line numbers are as I read them. `lightspeed.mjs`, `shop-mode.mjs`, `diary.mjs`, `book.mjs`, `quote.mjs`, `collect.mjs` and `settings-frame.mjs` had no uncommitted changes. `opening.mjs`, `setup.mjs`, `customer.mjs` and `account.mjs` were being changed by other helpers while I worked. I re-checked the lines I cite from them after their changes appeared, but their line numbers may move again. Lightspeed's own names, numbers and products are bracketed placeholders. There is still no Lightspeed test account (proof steps LS-01 to LS-09 pending). Nothing here says what Lightspeed's connection can or can't do. Where a finding depends on that, it says "unverified". Lightspeed's own till screens are Lightspeed's and aren't drawn, so the payment step itself isn't walked. Only what Wheelhouse tells the people on either side of it is walked.

**Not raised, on purpose.** Anything `lightspeed-ui-audit.md` raised that decision 10 settled, unless the story makes it worse. Everything walk-throughs 1–5 changed. "Mark ready for collection" staying on when the work order isn't in Lightspeed (decision 10, audit H3 option 1): H2 below accepts that and draws what happens at the counter next. Website management for a Lightspeed shop (decision 10, H1), except where the customer pages in this story still show a Basket. The "Bike is here" button on ready and collected job boards, which comes from the shared job page, not from journey 21. None of the recorded decisions is reopened. Where a finding touches one, it says which. M1 changes when the customer look-up runs, not its rule, and says so.

**Verdict.** The staff side of journey 21 is thorough. Each Lightspeed state on the job has its own strip, words and button. The money promise is exact. Handing over before payment shows is recorded. A price change can't reach Maya unseen. The breaks are where the story leaves those boards. **Maya's pages were never drawn for a Lightspeed shop.** Booking, the quote and the ready page are journey 3, 4 and 5's boards, and none of them uses the Lightspeed-shop switch. So Maya's booking page says "Deposit paid £[deposit]", her ready link offers "Pay £111.00 now", the header has a Basket, and there are two different "your bike is ready" pages (H1). **At the counter,** a job marked ready with no work order in Lightspeed leaves Jo with a bare "Hand over" and Maya with nothing she can pay against. If Jo rings it up by hand, Wheelhouse may later send a second work order for the same bike (H2). Around those: the customer look-up waits for approval, when nobody is at the desk, and a job with nothing to approve has no moment for its work order at all (M1). Nothing tells Jo how the Lightspeed till finds WH-1042 (M2). A wrong customer link can't be undone on any drawn screen (M3). "Ask Maya again" sends a quote that says something was added, not that a price went up (M4). A bike handed over unpaid can never be cleared (M5). The messages still state an amount to pay that Wheelhouse may not know (M6).

## The story as walked

Clicks are taps or clicks on the main path; typing is listed separately. The main path is a one-shop Lightspeed shop with payment checking working, Maya already linked to one clear Lightspeed customer, and nothing going wrong.

1. **Maya books.** `wb-home` → `bk-service` → `bk-bike` → `bk-when` → `bk-details` → `bk-request` (phone), as in walk-through 1, with no deposit step because Lightspeed shops take none (decision 9). **About 6–7 taps and 6 fields.** Every one of these boards is drawn as a shop that sells online and takes deposits (H1).
2. **Jo accepts and books in.** `diary` → request → Accept; Today → "Book in" → `job-book-in` (desktop). **About 5–6 clicks.** Nothing Lightspeed happens here (M1).
3. **Alex quotes.** `ls-part-search` (tablet/desktop): Lightspeed's products, one press to add ("3 in stock · checked 40 seconds ago"). Then "Send quote" as in journey 4. **About 9 taps.**
4. **Maya approves.** `dq-quote` (phone) → "Approve £111.00" → `dq-answered`. **2 taps.** Wheelhouse makes the work order: `ls-job-sent`, "In Lightspeed · work order [number] · Agreed £111.00 · Made when Maya approved".
5. **Alex does the work.** `job-mechanic`, checklist, "Mark ready for collection". **About 16 taps.** "Bike ready" goes to Maya by text.
6. **Maya opens the link.** It goes to the job's page. Drawn for a normal shop as `cp-summary` ("Pay £111.00 now") or `cp-summary-inshop` ("To pay when you collect"). Drawn for a Lightspeed shop as `ls-customer-ready`, a different page under "Your account" (H1). **1 tap.**
7. **Jo takes the payment.** Jo opens WH-1042 (`ls-job-unpaid`, desktop: "Waiting to be paid in Lightspeed · Maya pays at the Lightspeed till · Agreed £111.00"). She goes to the Lightspeed till, finds the work order (M2) and takes payment there (Lightspeed's screens, not walked). **2 clicks plus typing in Wheelhouse; Lightspeed's own clicks not counted.**
8. **Jo hands over.** If the payment shows, `ls-job-paid` → "Hand over": **1 click.** If it hasn't shown yet: "Hand over" → `ls-hand-over-unpaid` → "Check Lightspeed now" → `ls-hand-over-found` → "Hand over": **3 clicks.**
9. **Jack.** Nothing on the main path. On a bad day, Today's lines (`ls-today-down`, `ls-today-person`, `ls-today-unpaid`) are his, as Owner.

**Totals on the main path.** Maya: about 10 taps and 6 fields, with no payment in Wheelhouse. Jo: about 8–10 clicks in Wheelhouse, plus a trip to a second system to take payment. Alex: about 25 taps, most of them the checklist. Jack: none.
