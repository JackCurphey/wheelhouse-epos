# UX walk-through 4 — a new shop

Walked 2 Oct 2026 by the walk-through helper, as story 4 of `docs/decisions/2026-10-02-ux-walkthrough.md` (decision 6: walk every remaining story). This is not a UI audit. Each journey has had its own audit, screen by screen. This walk follows North Street Cycles from Citrus Lime to its first day on Wheelhouse, across four journeys, as the people setting it up, and looks for what breaks where one journey hands over to the next.

**The story.** Jack Lewis (Owner) brings North Street Cycles' data across from Citrus Lime and starts running alongside it (journey 9). He sets the shop up from the Getting started checklist on Today: shop details, a till, the card machine, staff, messages (journey 8). He invites Jo Taylor. She signs in, gets her till PIN and checks in at Till B1 for the first time (journey B, with journey 10's float check). Jack sets up the website and turns it on (journey 18), which the switch-over checklist asks for. Walked as Jack (desktop), Jo (a phone or computer to sign in, the desktop till to check in), and again as someone using a screen reader or only a keyboard, and as someone with low vision.

**How facts are handled.** Every finding names the screens (id and size) and the source line or decision it rests on. Line numbers are as I read them; several modules were being edited by other helpers at the same time, so lines may have shifted. Bracketed placeholders (`[n]`, `[float]`, `[payment provider]`, `[date]`) are unknowns, not errors. What Citrus Lime's exports contain is still unknown (Moving 2); I haven't assumed anything about them. Where I couldn't check something, I say so.

**Not raised, on purpose.** Anything a journey's own UI audit already covers, unless the story makes it worse. Everything walk-throughs 1–3 changed. WorkOS's own pages (sign in, accept an invitation, set a password), which stay WorkOS's (Signing in 2) and are drawn as one approximate board. Custom web addresses waiting on the business plan (ECOM-03, Website 7). Role labels: other helpers are changing these while I work (`setup.mjs`, `settings-frame.mjs`, `signin.mjs` and others have uncommitted changes). Jack Lewis is the Owner, and manager-view boards show "[Manager]". I've treated what's changing as their work, not a finding. `moving.mjs` has no uncommitted changes and still signs in as "Shop owner" (L1). None of the recorded decisions is reopened. Where a finding touches one, it says which. H2's recommended fix changes what one item of Moving 7's checklist means, and says so.

**Verdict.** Each journey works on its own. The move's three stages, the self-ticking checklists, the PIN-only check-in and the website's three-step start are all sound. The breaks are at the joins, and most come from one thing: for weeks, the shop runs Wheelhouse in practice beside Citrus Lime, and only the till knows that. The switch-over checklist asks Jack to move the website first, but once the website is on, it takes real orders and bookings that Citrus Lime never sees, and the weekly check can't match again (H2). Automatic messages have no practice mode, so real imported customers can be texted about practice work (H3). The Getting started checklist tells Jack "you can sell now" while every till is in practice (M1), and the float check and Close the day aren't drawn for practice (M4). For Jo, nothing is drawn between the invite email and the till: no first sign-in, no "get your PIN" step, and the PIN screen doesn't tell someone without a PIN what to do (H1). Someone with no email can't join at all (M2), and an invite that expires simply disappears (M3).

## The story as walked

Clicks are taps or clicks on the main path; typing, file picking and PIN digits are listed separately. The main path is a one-shop business at Bolton, Jack as Owner, Jo as Staff.

1. **Jack starts the move.** `fr-today` (desktop): Getting started, with "Moving from another system?" top right (`setup.mjs` line 393). → `mv-start`: "Download these from Citrus Lime, then drop them in together". "Choose files" → `mv-progress` starts by itself → `mv-summary` "Here's what came across" → "Look at the [n]" → `mv-fix`: Fix or Leave it out on each row → `mv-sorted` → "Next: run alongside". **About 4 clicks, plus one per problem row, plus downloading the files from Citrus Lime.** What happens to a left-out row at the next refresh isn't drawn (M5).
2. **Running alongside.** `mv-alongside`: weekly refresh, "Every till is in practice". `mv-today-refresh` puts "Time to refresh from Citrus Lime" on Today each week. **2 clicks a week, plus files and four typed figures** (`mv-check`).
3. **Jack works through Getting started.** `fr-today`: "Your till is ready, so you can sell now" (line 400), although every till is in practice (M1). "Start" → `fr-step` (Payments, card machine) → "Next: Add your staff" → Staff and roles → "+ Invite someone" → `set-staff-invite`: email, role, "Send the invite". The till itself: `till-setup`, site and number already picked, "Make this computer Till B1". **About 2–3 clicks a step, about 16 for the six steps left, plus typing.** Nothing shows the invite once it's sent (M3).
4. **Jo joins.** Invite email → WorkOS's accept-the-invitation page → (not drawn) her first screen in Wheelhouse → her name → `your-settings` → "Change PIN" → `pin-change` → "Keep this PIN". **About 6 clicks, plus a password, across two steps with no screen** (H1).
5. **Jo checks in.** `till-checkin` (desktop): "Enter your PIN", 4 digits, checks itself. As first in to take payments, `op-float-check`: "Hello, Jo … The drawer should have £[float]" → "Looks right". **1 click, plus 4 digits.** In practice, which drawer and which float isn't drawn (M4). The practice band appears only after check-in (`mv-practice-sale`).
6. **Jo makes her practice sale.** `mv-practice-sale`, `mv-practice-card`: "Pretend it's paid". **About 3 clicks.** This ticks "Everyone has made a practice sale" on `mv-ready`.
7. **Jack sets up the website.** Website → `ws-start-which` "Next" → `ws-start-look` (logo, colour) "Next" → `ws-start-products` "Make my website" → `ws-editor-first` → edits → `ws-page` "Turn it on" (which also publishes). Payments: "Open Online orders settings" → `ws-pay-none` "Connect [payment provider]" → `ws-pay-connected` "Make a test payment". **About 8 clicks, plus a logo and the edits.** Turning on buying online asks the first-step question again (M7). Nothing counts products without photos (M6). Turning the website on during the move breaks the weekly check (H2).

**Totals on the main path.** Jack: about 30 clicks across the move, Getting started and the website, plus downloads, typing and one click per problem row, and 2 clicks a week while running alongside. Jo: about 10 clicks, plus a password and 4 PIN digits, with two steps that have no screen.

## High

**H1 — `set-staff-invite`, `your-settings`, `pin-change`, `till-checkin` (desktop): nothing is drawn between Jo's invite and her first check-in, and the screens that are drawn assume she already has a PIN.**
- The invite says "They get an email to sign in" and "They choose their own till PIN" (`setup.mjs` lines 245, 249). Signing in 7 says Wheelhouse picks each PIN; nobody chooses one.
- After WorkOS's accept-the-invitation page (Signing in 2, not drawn), no board shows where a new member of staff lands, or tells them to get a PIN. Owner setup audit M20 kept a rule ("Jo sets a new one at next sign-in"), but no board draws it.
- Your settings only shows a PIN that's already set: "••••", announced "PIN is set", "Change PIN" (`app-map.mjs` line 173). `pin-change` ends "Your old PIN stops working when you keep this one" (`signin.mjs` line 132).
- At the till, `till-checkin` says "Enter your PIN … It works even when the internet is down" (line 111). It says nothing to someone who doesn't have a PIN, and the till can't give her one: it stays signed in as the till (`till-setup`).

*Why it matters:* this is the moment the story exists for. Jo arrives for her first shift, goes to the till and can't check in. Nothing on the till tells her why or what to do. The only way to a PIN is a settings pop-up on another device that nothing points her to. That also holds up "Everyone has made a practice sale", which switch-over waits for. *Fix (touches Signing in 2, 6 and 7; none reopened: Wheelhouse still picks the PIN, and only Jo sees it):*
1. Jo's first sign-in, after WorkOS's page, opens "Your till PIN" straight away. This is the `pin-change` pop-up in a first-time version: the heading "Your till PIN", no "old PIN" line, "Keep this PIN" and "Give me a different one", and "Skip for now" for someone who never uses the till. Then she lands in her room. Your settings shows "No PIN yet" with "Get your PIN" until she has one. The till's PIN screen adds one line under the pad: "No PIN yet? Sign in to Wheelhouse on a phone or computer — it gives you one." The invite's note reads "Wheelhouse gives them a till PIN when they first sign in." A cleared PIN works the same way (audit M20's rule, now drawn).
2. Only the Your settings "No PIN yet" state and the till line, with no first-sign-in pop-up. Fewer boards, but Jo still has to find Your settings on her own.

Recommend 1.

**H2 — `mv-ready`, `mv-alongside`, `mv-check-result` (desktop); `ws-page`, `ws-address-steps`, `ws-pay-tested`: the switch-over checklist asks for the website to be moved before switch-over, but a website that's on during run alongside takes real orders and bookings that Citrus Lime never sees, and the weekly check can't match again.**
- The checklist's fourth item is "The website is moved — Your website runs on Wheelhouse, not Citrus Lime", with "Open Website" (`moving.mjs` line 184; Moving 7). Nothing says what ticks it: turning the website on, or the shop's own address pointing to it.
- Everything else about the move is practice: "Practice: not real money … cleared on switch-over day" (`till.mjs` practice band; Moving 6). The website has no practice mode. Once it's on and payments are connected, customers can buy ("Stock is held for the customer as soon as they've paid", `online.mjs` line 305) and book a repair from it (Website 11's ready-made home page has Book a repair).
- The weekly refresh brings Citrus Lime's changes in, and "where both changed the same thing: Citrus Lime's kept until switch-over" (`moving.mjs` line 128). An online sale takes stock off in Wheelhouse. If Citrus Lime also sells that product that week, the refresh puts Citrus Lime's figure back and the online sale's stock change is lost. Citrus Lime never hears of the online sale, or of a booking made on the website.
- The weekly check compares sales total, number of sales and stock value, and "a week counts only when all four match" (Moving 9 H2). Real online sales are in Wheelhouse's figures, not Citrus Lime's, so after the website goes on no week can match. The first checklist item ("matched 2 weeks in a row") can then never tick again.
- The shop's own address: "your old website stops showing on it. Turn your website on first" (`website.mjs` line 373). Custom addresses are frozen (ECOM-03). If "moved" means the shop's own address, the item can't tick until that's decided.

*Why it matters:* Jack does what the checklist asks, and the move then can't finish. Meanwhile a customer can pay online for a pump that Citrus Lime then sells across the counter, or book a repair into a diary nobody is looking at. *Fix (touches Moving 6 and 7, Website 7 and audit H2):*
1. The website is made ready before switch-over and turned on during the switch-over morning. The checklist row becomes "The website is ready", ticking when its three-step start is done, payments are connected and the test payment worked, and its starting wording has been checked. Its line reads "It goes on during switch-over morning". `mv-morning` gains a third step, "Turn your website on" (with "and point [your address] to it — this can take up to a day" when the shop uses its own address). Until then the website stays "Only your staff can see it", as `ws-page` already draws. Citrus Lime's website keeps selling until the day. This changes what Moving 7's fourth item means, from moved to ready, which is why it's marked.
2. The website goes on during run alongside but can't take orders or bookings until switch-over: buying online and online booking stay off ("Not taking online orders right now", the existing words), and switch-over morning turns them on. This keeps Moving 7 as it is, but the shop's online sales stop for the weeks of running alongside, and stock shown is up to a week old.
3. Leave it. Nothing to build, but the move can't finish once the website is on.

Recommend 1.

**H3 — `set-msg-list` (desktop), `mv-alongside`, `mv-summary`, `fr-today`: automatic messages have no practice mode, so imported customers can be texted about practice work.**
- Messages starts with "23 on" (`settings-frame.mjs` line 238). Among them are Bike ready, Quote to approve, the booking messages and Service reminder, timed on each service (`setup.mjs` lines 313-334).
- During run alongside the customers are real: they came across from Citrus Lime (`mv-summary`). Practice work exists: the refresh never overwrites "practice jobs, notes" (Moving 3). Practice is defined only for the till and reports: "Practice sales … stay out of reports and the weekly check" (Moving 6; `reports.mjs`).
- I searched the generator for "practice" outside `moving.mjs`: only `till.mjs`, `reports.mjs` and `journeys.mjs` match. No board says whether a message is sent while the shop runs alongside.
- Service reminders go "Only to customers who said yes" (`setup.mjs` line 332). Whether a customer's yes or no comes across from Citrus Lime isn't known (Moving 2).
- Getting started's message step ticks "when you've looked at Messages" (line 374). It asks nothing about this.

*Why it matters:* a customer whose bike is in for a real repair, booked in Citrus Lime, gets "your bike is ready" from a practice job someone tried in Wheelhouse. Or every imported customer gets a service reminder the week the data arrives. The shop's promise that practice isn't real breaks in front of customers. *Fix (touches Moving 6, Owner setup 14, Account and reminders 2 and 5):*

Settings › Messages carries a line, "Running alongside Citrus Lime: no messages go to customers until switch-over day", and where a message would have gone, the job or booking says "Not sent — practice". Staff can still send a message by hand. Switch-over morning turns messages on, and nothing is sent for anything that happened before (no backlog). Service reminders count from imported history, but the first ones go out after switch-over, only to customers whose yes came across or who say yes again.
2. Messages go only to the shop's own number and email while in practice, so staff see what customers would get. Good for testing the wording, but it needs a shop contact for every kind and floods the shop's phone.
3. Leave it. Nothing to build; Jack has to switch 23 messages off by hand and remember to switch them back on.
