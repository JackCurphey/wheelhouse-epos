# UX walk-through 7 — an owner with two shops

Walked 2 Oct 2026 by the walk-through helper, as story 7 of `docs/decisions/2026-10-02-ux-walkthrough.md` (decision 6: walk every remaining story). This is the last of the seven. It is not a UI audit; journeys 19, 20 and 17 had their own (`sites-ui-audit.md`, `oversight-ui-audit.md`, `reports-ui-audit.md`), and every recommendation in them was taken (Multiple sites 9, Management oversight 6, Reports and accounts 8). This walk follows one business as it becomes two shops, as the people living it, and looks for what breaks where one shop hands over to the other.

**The story.** Jack Lewis (Owner) runs North Street Cycles at Bolton (code B, tills B1–B3). He adds a second shop, "[Second site]", in Settings, and works through its checklist on Today: its tills, who works there, its stock. Jo Taylor (Staff) works at both shops: Bolton on Tue, Wed and Sat, and at [Second site] on days not yet chosen (`ms-person`). At Bolton, Jo books a customer's job into [Second site]'s workshop; it arrives there as a request, and someone at [Second site] accepts it (Multiple sites 12). On Thursday 17 September, Till [code]1 at [Second site] has sales waiting and its float was short, while Jack is looking at Bolton. Jack switches shops, compares the two shops in Reports, and checks the activity log and signed-in devices (journey 20). A "[Manager]" placeholder appears where a manager's view is drawn. Walked as Jack (desktop), Jo at Bolton and at [Second site] (desktop), and again as someone using a screen reader or only a keyboard, and as someone with low vision (phone and tablet sizes).

**How facts are handled.** Every finding names the screens (id and size) and the source line or decision it rests on. Line numbers are as I read them. `sites.mjs`, `oversight.mjs` and `signin.mjs` had no uncommitted changes. `diary.mjs` and `journeys.mjs` started changing while I worked (walk-through 6 fixes: the job's "Take payment" note, Lightspeed boards); I re-checked the lines I cite from them afterwards. `opening.mjs`, `reports.mjs`, `setup.mjs`, `stock.mjs`, `settings-frame.mjs` and `customer.mjs` had uncommitted changes from other helpers (walk-throughs 5 and 6 fixes: Cycle to Work lines on Today and in Reports, the Lightspeed message wording, the Cycle to Work stock-take note). None of those changes touch a line this report relies on, but their line numbers may move. The second shop has no real name, address, code or figures, so it is "[Second site]" throughout, and nothing here invents them.

**Not raised, on purpose.** Everything walk-throughs 1–6 changed, including walk-through 1 M1 (booking starts with the shop the website remembered). Everything the three UI audits raised and the decisions settled, unless the story makes it worse. In particular, sites audit M13 made the "All shops" Today list [Second site]'s lines only, so that it agrees with Bolton's own Today. H1 below accepts that and is about something else: Bolton's Today saying nothing about the other shop. Decision 11's "small type under the page title" is kept; L2 only asks for it to be easier to read. None of the recorded decisions is reopened. Where a finding touches one, it says which.

**Verdict.** Each shop on its own is well drawn. The switcher is named, announced and keyboard-ready. "All shops" puts both shops on one grid, and the reports that matter most to an accountant (Sales, Takings, VAT) have "All shops" versions. The breaks are where one shop has to tell the other something. **An alert at the shop Jack isn't looking at never reaches him.** Bolton's Today tells the owner "Nothing needs you right now" while [Second site]'s float is short and its sales haven't sent, and the new shop's checklist lives only on a page he has to switch to (H1). **A job booked into the other shop's workshop is drawn with Bolton's diary.** Jo picks Bolton's time, Bolton's mechanic and a Bolton hook. The request that arrives at [Second site] is a different customer's job, and nothing shows Bolton or the customer hearing the answer (H2). Around those: the shops can only be compared in three of the eight reports (M1). A till can't be moved between shops, and a new shop's tills can't be numbered (M2). A new shop can show to customers before it can serve them (M3). The activity log and the alerts don't keep to their shop (M4). The transfer boards offer a third shop that doesn't exist, and stocking a new shop is one product at a time (M5). The messages don't say which shop (M6).

## The story as walked

Clicks are taps or clicks on the main path; typing is listed separately.

1. **Jack adds the shop.** Settings → Office → Shop and sites (`ms-sites`, desktop) → "+ Add a shop" (`ms-add-shop`) → "Copy hours from Bolton" → "Add the shop". **About 6 clicks and 4 fields.** He lands on [Second site]'s Today, "Getting [Second site] ready" (`ms-today-new`), with three steps left. What customers see now isn't said (M3).
2. **Jack gets it ready.** "Open Staff and roles" → each person → tick "[Second site]" under Works at, and a mechanic's days (`ms-person`) → Done: **about 4 clicks a person**. "Register a till" must be done on the till's own computer (`till-setup`), where the till numbers on offer are Bolton's (M2). "Send from Bolton" opens a send pop-up for one product at a time (`tr-send`, M5). **Not countable as drawn.**
3. **Jack switches shops.** Switcher → a shop (`ms-switch-open` → `ms-switched`): **2 clicks.** "All shops" (`ms-today-all`): **2 clicks.** Until he does, Bolton's Today says nothing about [Second site] (H1).
4. **Jo books a job into [Second site]'s workshop.** Bolton's diary → New job → "Workshop at: [Second site]" → customer → work → "Send request to [Second site]" (`ms-job-other-shop`, desktop): **about 6 clicks and a search.** The time and mechanic shown are Bolton's (H2).
5. **At [Second site], someone accepts it.** Diary → the request (`ms-request-from-shop`) → "Accept": **2 clicks.** The request shown is a different job (H2). Bolton's side of the answer isn't drawn.
6. **Jack compares the shops.** Reports → switcher → All shops → Sales (`rp-sales-all`): **about 4 clicks.** Takings and VAT the same (`rp-takings-all`, `rp-vat-all`). Workshop, Margin and stock value, and Discounts and refunds have no All shops version (M1).
7. **Jack checks on staff and devices.** A Today alert → the log filtered to it (`ops-log-filtered`): **1 click.** Reports → Activity log (`ops-log-all`): **2 clicks.** Settings → Office → Staff and roles → Signed-in devices (`ops-devices`): **4 clicks.** The log and the devices list don't keep to their shop (M4).

**Totals on the main path.** Jack: about 20 clicks to add and switch, plus about 4 per person; stocking the new shop isn't countable. Jo: about 6 clicks to send a request. The person at [Second site]: 2 clicks to accept.

## High

**H1 — `ms-switch-open`, `op-today` (desktop), `ms-switched`, `ms-today-all`, `ms-today-new`, `auth-site` (desktop, phone): an alert at the shop Jack isn't looking at never reaches him. Bolton's Today tells the owner "Nothing needs you right now" while [Second site] has three problems.**
- `ms-switch-open` (desktop, rendered) is Jack's Bolton Today on Thursday 17 September: "Needs attention · Nothing needs you right now" (`sites.mjs` line 177 calls `today({ as: OWNER })`; `opening.mjs` line 187).
- On the same date, [Second site]'s Today (`ms-switched`, desktop, rendered) lists "Till [code]1 has [n] sales waiting to send", "Till [code]1's float was £[n] short this morning" and a ready bike left since [date] (`SECOND_LINES`, `sites.mjs` lines 62-66). `ms-today-all` lists the same three.
- Nothing on Bolton's Today points at them. The switcher's rows show only "[Address] · [n] tills" (`shopOption`, line 52). `today()` has no line for another shop (`today()`, `opening.mjs` from line 99; I searched the file for "Second site" and found nothing).
- The new shop's checklist is drawn only on [Second site]'s Today (`ms-today-new`, lines 158-164). Once Jack switches back to Bolton, nothing reminds him that three steps are left.
- Signing in doesn't help. "Where are you working today?" (`auth-site`, desktop, rendered; `signin.mjs` lines 54-59) offers Bolton or [Second site], with no "All shops" for the owner and no sign that either shop needs anything.

*Why it matters:* the owner is the one person who should hear about a short float at either shop. The page he opens most tells him there's nothing, at the exact moment one of his shops has a cash difference and a till that hasn't sent its sales. *Fix, no choice (touches Multiple sites 1 and 6, and sites audit M13; none reopened: the page still shows the chosen shop, and the All shops list keeps only [Second site]'s lines):*
- For anyone who can see more than one shop, Bolton's Needs attention ends with one line: "[Second site] · 3 things need attention", with "See them", which switches to [Second site]. With nothing at the other shop, the line doesn't show. While a shop's checklist is unfinished, the line reads "[Second site] · 3 steps to get it ready". Draw it on `ms-switch-open` (a new `today({ otherShops })` option; `op-today` itself stays a one-shop board).
- The switcher's row for a shop with something waiting adds "3 need attention" under its address (`shopOption`).
- `auth-site` gets "All shops" as a third card, "Owners, and managers at two or more shops", and each shop card shows "[n] need attention" when something does.
- No new boards.

**H2 — `ms-job-other-shop`, `ms-request-from-shop` (desktop, tablet, phone): a job booked into the other shop's workshop is drawn with Bolton's diary, and the request that arrives at [Second site] is a different job. Neither shop's next step after the answer is drawn.**
- `ms-job-other-shop` is Bolton's ordinary new-job form with a "Workshop at" choice on top (`newJobOtherShopAt`, `diary.mjs` lines 2962-2965). It passes the same `NEW_JOB_OPTS` as the plain `new-job` board (line 2136). So with "[Second site]" chosen (desktop, rendered), the form still shows "Tue 15 Sep · 10:00 · Alex Morgan", "Mechanic chosen automatically: most free time on Tuesday", "Only 30 minutes free at 10:00 — this job needs 60", Bolton's hooks ("Hook 1 … Front window", "Storage slots are on for this shop"), and the starting statuses "Bike is here" and "The bike is here now".
- Jo can't see [Second site]'s free times, so whatever time she tells the customer comes from the wrong diary. "Bike is here" would put a bike that is standing in Bolton on [Second site]'s workshop list.
- `ms-request-from-shop` (desktop, rendered) is the generic Sam Reed request with a "From Bolton · booked by Jo Taylor at [time]" line (`requestFromShopAt`, lines 2966-2969; `requestNewBody`, line 1816). It shows "Sam Reed · Specialized Sirrus · Brake service · Requested Friday 18 September", "No message from the customer", and mechanics "Alex", "Jo" and "Shared queue". The form Jo filled in was Maya Patel, Standard service, Tue 15 Sep, with the note "Rear brake squeals and feels weak." The diary behind it is Bolton's example week (its waiting list shows Oliver Chen and Aisha Khan, Bolton's customers). Whether Alex works at [Second site] isn't established anywhere.
- The person accepting it is Jo Taylor (Staff), the same person who sent it from Bolton. `journeys.mjs` line 1150 calls the board "Mechanic".
- The form says "you and the customer hear back" (`shopPick`, line 2962), and decision 12 says both shops and the customer hear the answer. No board shows Bolton hearing it, and no message is shown naming [Second site] (M6).

*Why it matters:* this is the one hand-off between the two shops' workshops, and Jack chose it himself. As drawn, the customer may be told a time [Second site] never had, the request arrives looking like someone else's job, and Jo never finds out whether it was taken. *Fix (touches Multiple sites 12; not reopened):*
1. Redraw both boards as one job, and draw Bolton's answer. On `ms-job-other-shop`, with "[Second site]" chosen, the right-hand panel shows [Second site]'s diary: "[Second site]'s next free 60 minutes: [day] [time] · [Mechanic]", worded "Asked for — [Second site] confirms". The hooks go. The starting status is only "Booked", with "The customer brings the bike to [Second site]". The customer's note goes with the request. `ms-request-from-shop` becomes Maya Patel's request: Standard service, the asked-for time, her note, "From Bolton · booked by Jo Taylor", [Second site]'s [Mechanic], and someone at [Second site] signed in rather than Jo. Draw `ms-request-answered`, Bolton's diary with the sent request in its waiting list: "Sent to [Second site] · accepted for [day] [time]" (or "another time offered", or "declined: [reason]"), with "Open". One new board.
2. Fix only the two existing boards and say in a note that Bolton sees the answer in its waiting list. No new board, but the hand-back stays undrawn and decision 12's "the shop that booked it hears the answer" can't be checked.

Recommend 1.

## Medium

**M1 — `rp-sales-all`, `rp-takings-all`, `rp-vat-all`, `rp-workshop`, `rp-margin`, `rp-discounts` (desktop): the shops can be compared in only three of the eight reports, and none says when a shop started.**
- Sales, Takings and cash-ups and VAT take a shop and have "All shops" versions (`reports.mjs` lines 150-153, 212-213, 236-238; `rp-sales-all`, desktop, rendered: "Takings by shop" with Bolton and [Second site] rows).
- `margin()`, `workshop()` and `discounts()` are written for Bolton only: "North Street Cycles, Bolton" in their titles (lines 261, 286, 296), with no site option. Reports and accounts decision 8 (audit M13) says "Takings and VAT get All shops versions, the others a shop column". That column isn't drawn on any of them.
- The workshop is what an owner with two workshops compares first: jobs, turnaround, how full. `rp-workshop` (desktop, rendered) lists "How full each mechanic was" with Alex Morgan and Jo Taylor. Jo works at both shops, and the report doesn't say whose hours are counted.
- A job booked at Bolton and done at [Second site] (H2) isn't said to count at either shop.
- A shop added mid-period is compared against "the same days last week", when it didn't exist. `rp-sales-empty` draws "nothing to compare with" for a whole shop, but not for one row of two.

*Why it matters:* "comparing the shops in Reports" is the reason to have "All shops" in Reports at all. As drawn, the owner can compare money but not workshops, margins or discounts. *Fix, no choice (touches Reports and accounts 8; it draws what M13 already decided):* draw `rp-workshop-all`: the Workshop report for All shops, with a Shop column in each table, and "How full" split by shop for a person who works at both ("Jo Taylor · Bolton [n] of [n] hours · [Second site] [n] of [n]"). A note says a job counts at the shop whose workshop did it. `margin()` and `discounts()` take a site, so their All shops versions exist for the canvas to link (not drawn as boards). On `rp-sales-all`, [Second site]'s row reads "Opened [date] — nothing to compare before then". One new board.

**M2 — `ms-tills`, `till-setup`, `ops-devices` (desktop): a till can't be moved between shops, and a new shop's tills can't be numbered.**
- The Tills list (`ms-tills`, desktop, rendered; `sites.mjs` lines 167-172) has no action on a till, only "+ Add a till" for each shop. I searched the generator for "move this till", "move a till", "remove till" and "retire" and found no board for it.
- "Set up this till" (`till-setup`, desktop, rendered; `signin.mjs` lines 83-87) has "Site" pills (Bolton, [Second site]) and "Till number" pills that are always B1, B2 and B3. With [Second site] chosen, they don't change to its code. Bolton already has B1–B3 (`ms-tills`), so the board offers numbers that are taken.
- What happens to a till moved from Bolton to [Second site] isn't said: its sales still waiting, its day not yet closed, its float, its receipt numbers (B3-…), and Today's "This till is Bolton's" banner (`ms-till-other`).

*Why it matters:* lending a till to the new shop is the most likely way the second shop gets its first till. Nothing drawn tells Jack how, or what happens to the day's money on it. *Fix, no choice (touches Multiple sites 8 and 9 (H2: a till sells for its own shop); not reopened):* each till in `ms-tills` gets "…" with "Move to [Second site]…" and "Stop using this till…". Draw the move as `ms-till-move`: "Move Till B3 to [Second site]?", with "Its day is closed first, and any sales waiting send first. It becomes Till [code]3. Old receipts keep B3." In `till-setup`, choosing a shop shows that shop's free numbers, and taken numbers say "In use". One new board.

**M3 — `ms-add-shop`, `ms-today-new` (desktop): a new shop can show to customers before it can serve them.** The form says "Stock starts at zero" (`sites.mjs` line 155) and the checklist covers tills, staff and stock (lines 160-163). Neither says when [Second site] appears on the website's "Our shops", in booking's "Which shop?" (`ms-book-shop`) or as a collection shop for online orders (`on-choose-shop`). I found no "opening soon" or hidden state in `browse.mjs`, `book.mjs`, `online.mjs` or `website.mjs`. Until someone works in its workshop, its booking has no free times, and with no stock, "Collect from [Second site]" has nothing to collect. *Why it matters:* a customer can pick the new shop the day Jack types its name, then find nothing to book or a shop that isn't open yet. *Fix (touches Multiple sites 5 and 7; not reopened):*
1. A new shop stays hidden from customers until Jack shows it. The checklist gets a fifth step, "Show it to customers: on your website, in booking and for collecting online orders", with "Show [Second site] to customers". The form's note says "Customers don't see it until you choose to." Changes to `ms-add-shop` and `ms-today-new` only. No new boards.
2. It shows straight away, and booking says "No free times yet" until someone works there. Nothing to switch on, but customers meet an empty shop.

Recommend 1.

- Bolton's log (`ops-log`, "North Street Cycles, Bolton") includes Jack's "[Product] · −[n] · stock take" line, which in the All shops version is tagged "[Second site]" (`oversight.mjs` line 80: the shop is only set when `shops` is true). So Bolton's log shows another shop's line with no tag.
- "Price changed · Shimano brake pads B05S-RX · £28.00 → £[£]" is tagged "Bolton" (line 75; `ops-log-all`, rendered). - Signed-in devices lists two tills, "Till B1" at Bolton and "[Till]" at [Second site] (`oversight.mjs` line 121; `ops-devices`, rendered). The Tills list has five, grouped by shop: B1–B3 and [code]1–[code]2 (`ms-tills`).

*Why it matters:* the log's whole point is to trust what it says about who did what, where. The [Second site] stock-take line leaves `ops-log`. A change to an all-shops price is tagged "All shops", and its alert shows once on each shop's Today, cleared everywhere by one "Seen" (a price set "only at [Second site]" is tagged and alerts there). The devices list groups tills by shop, with the same names as the Tills list. No new boards.

**M5 — `tr-send`, `tr-incoming`, `tr-receive`, `tr-today-short`, `ms-today-new` (desktop): the transfer boards name a "[Site 2]" and a "[Site 3]", and stocking a new shop is one product at a time.**

The checklist's "Send from Bolton" opens it from Deliveries and orders with [Second site] already chosen. No new boards.

**M6 — `set-msg-edit` (desktop): the messages don't say which shop.** "Bike ready"'s preview reads "your Trek Domane AL 3 is ready to collect from North Street Cycles" (`setup.mjs` line 396), with "[Shop name]" in the wording (line 394).
