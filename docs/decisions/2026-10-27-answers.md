# Jack's answers, 27 Oct 2026

Questions from Deliveries and orders (1 to 3) and Close the day (4 to 8),
answered by Jack in chat on 27 Oct.
Each answer names what it unblocks.

## 1. Closing an order that has partly arrived

The question: an order has partly arrived (say 3 of 5 brake pads came) and
the supplier won't send the rest. Until now Wheelhouse refused to close an
order once anything on it had arrived.

**Mark the rest not coming** (Jack: "Mark the rest not coming"). Close the
order:

- keeps what arrived in stock and on the order's history;
- marks the rest as not coming;
- takes the order off "still to come".

Nothing that arrived is undone. Jack chose this over "keep refusing" and
over "close, and ask what to do each time".

What it unblocks: Close the order on a partly delivered order, as drawn on
`rs-order-ordered` (`openspec/changes/close-part-delivered`).

Nothing else in Deliveries and orders waits on it.

## 3. The float check starts at the till page until PIN check-in

**Opening the till page starts the float check** (Jack: "Opening the till
page"). Opening the shop decision 2 has it pop up for the first person who
checks in at the till with their PIN; PIN check-in (WP-1.7) isn't built yet.
Until it is, the first person who takes payments to open the till page each
day is asked, once per shop per day. When PIN check-in lands, it moves to the
check-in, as drawn. Chosen over keeping the float check off until WP-1.7.

What it unblocks: the float check (WP-3.3, slice B) can be played on the test
copy now (`openspec/changes/opening-float-check`).

## 4. The card check waits for a real card machine

The question: Close the day's fifth step checks the till's card payments
against the card machine's own total (journey 11 decision 6), and no card
machine is connected to Wheelhouse, so there is no machine total to check.

**Leave it out until a real card machine is connected; then build it as
drawn** (`eod-card`). The end-of-day report still shows the till's own card
total.

What it unblocks: Close the day without the card step
(`openspec/changes/close-the-day`).

## 5. A count below the float: put the difference in

The question: cash-up decision 4 says leave the shop's float in the drawer
and bank the rest. What happens when the count is less than the float?

**Close the day says "Put £[n] in to make the float up to £[float]" before
finishing**, and banks £0. Jack chose this, knowing it isn't drawn, over
"bank nothing, and tomorrow's float check shows it short". It follows the
page's existing wording and patterns.

What it unblocks: the banking step for a drawer below the float.

## 6. A sale after the day is closed counts in the next day

The question: the till isn't stopped once the day is closed. Which day's
takings does a sale made after the close go in?

**The next day's takings and drawer.** A closed day's report never
changes. So a day's figures run from the previous close (or the start of
the day, when there is none) to this close: the window comes from the
close's saved time, not the calendar day.

What it unblocks: the day's figures in Close the day.

## 7. Takings is till money only

The question: some sales in the old record were paid online, as a Shopify
order or a deposit paid online when booking. Are they in a till's takings?

**No. Takings is till money only.** Shopify orders and online deposits (the
tenders `Shopify` and `Deposit paid online`) go on their own "Online money"
line, as drawn on `eod-z`, not in Takings, Card, Cash or Other.

What it unblocks: the end-of-day report's figures.

## 8. A float nobody checked: Looks right at closing

The question: the day can't close without this morning's float check. If
nobody checked it, what does Close the day ask?

**Only "Looks right"**: the shop's set float, one press. Never Count it at
closing.

What it unblocks: closing a day whose float wasn't checked.

## 9. Trust the morning check

The question: a review found the drawer's expected cash added some cash
twice. It started from this morning's float check, which counts what is in
the drawer, then added every cash sale since the previous close. A cash sale
made after yesterday's close was already in this morning's count, so it was
added again (the review's example: a start of £172.00 and £22.00 of such
cash gave £194.00 expected, when the drawer really held £172.00).

**Trust the morning check** (Jack: "Trust the morning check"). For a day nobody closed, the drawings win instead (29 Oct answer 5, `2026-10-29-answers.md`). What the
drawer should hold is this morning's start (what was counted, or the shop's
float for "Looks right"), plus cash sales after the float check, minus cash
taken out after it. Cash left over from a day nobody closed then shows as
over tonight, so it's seen, not hidden. The report's Takings, Card, Cash,
Other and Online money still cover everything no close has counted yet
(answer 6).

What it unblocks: the drawer's expected figure in Close the day.
