# A request cancelled after the cut-off — Jack's decision (24 Oct 2026)

**Asked** (while building deposits part 3, `openspec/changes/archive/2026-10-24-deposit-refunds`):
a request the shop hasn't confirmed yet, cancelled by the customer after
"Free to cancel until", kept the deposit, as step 4's words say ("After that,
or if you don't come, the shop keeps it"). A request for tomorrow is past its
cut-off the moment it's sent.

**Decision** (Jack, 24 Oct: "I think a shop should not be able to keep a
deposit if they haven't gotten back to the customer and confirmed the job or
not"): **a request the shop hasn't confirmed, cancelled by the customer, gets
its deposit back, whenever it's cancelled.** Only a confirmed booking keeps
the deposit after the cut-off. A time the shop offered and the customer
hasn't accepted counts as not confirmed.
