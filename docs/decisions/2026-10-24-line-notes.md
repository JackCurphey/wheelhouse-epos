# A line's note is for staff (Jack, 24 Oct 2026)

**Question:** who should see the note on a line of a job's work and parts?

**Jack's answer:** "Staff only (Recommended)".

**What it means:**
- The note is for the workshop. When a line comes from an approved quote,
  the reason the customer was given is copied in, so the mechanic sees why
  the line is there.
- The customer only ever sees what the quote said. A note written or
  changed later never reaches the customer's pages or the receipt.

**Built with it, following the job checklist** (decided while building,
`decided-while-building.md`):
- anyone signed in can tick Done or write a note, and the latest change
  wins;
- who ticked isn't recorded (that waits for PINs, WP-1.7);
- ticks and notes can't change once the work is finished, until it's
  reopened;
- a note is added with "+ Add note".
