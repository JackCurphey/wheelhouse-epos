# Service checklists

The full service checklist is drawn (`job-checklist`, ten items) and Jack
decided on 27 Sep (`2026-09-27-workshop-day-review.md` item 39) that it
"appears only for jobs whose service has a checklist (a general service or the
bigger services) — not, for example, a hub service". Nothing said how a shop
chooses which services have one, or who writes the items.

## Answer

1. **Each shop writes its own checklists in Settings and chooses which
   services have each one, with a ready-made template to start from.**
   Jack, 22 Oct: "I'd kind of like 2, so that each shop writes its own
   checklist in settings and then chooses which services have it. I would
   also like a default 10 for a full service, for example, so that if a shop
   doesn't want to have to write it out for a full service, they can just get
   a template to use."

   The template is the drawn ten: Frame & fork, Wheels & tyres, Tyre
   pressure, Brakes bled & adjusted, Gears indexed, Chain & drivetrain,
   Bottom bracket, Headset, Cables & housing, Bolts torqued. The Settings
   page isn't drawn yet; it is drawn for Jack's approval before it is built.
