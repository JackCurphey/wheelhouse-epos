# Jack's answers, 12 Nov 2026: Reports, the fourth part (WP-5.3)

Questions from writing the spec for the fourth part of WP-5.3, Reports and accounts (`openspec/changes/reports-accounts-and-reopen`): reopening a closed day, and connecting accounts software (Xero or QuickBooks, built against a pretend one) so each closed day goes across as one summary. Jack answered on the evening of 12 Nov, option 1 on each, and the Front desk session passed the answers on. Earlier parts' answers: `2026-11-10-reports-answers.md`, `2026-11-11-reports-part-2-answers.md`, `2026-11-11-reports-part-3-answers.md`.

## 1. What a re-close counts after a day is reopened

The question: a reopened day keeps the sales its first close counted. A sale made on that day and saved after the first close was never in it. Should the new close take it?

**The answer: option 1.** It stays for the next close, as every sale made after a close does today. A closed day never grows after the fact, and a reopened day can still miss that late sale.

## 2. What goes in the summary that Wheelhouse doesn't record yet

The question: refunds, discounts given, the VAT of old-record sales and a sale line's category at the time of sale aren't recorded yet (WP-3.1, Mark's step C).

**The answer: option 1.** Send each day with what is recorded, and show each unrecorded line as "not recorded" in that day's log entry, for the accountant to fill in. Days always go across and nothing is held up; the books get VAT-inclusive sales for old-record days until step C, which the accountant corrects. This is the same rule as the VAT report's (part 2, answer 3).

## 3. Online sales on the account choices

The question: a day's online money is "separate from the tills" and "goes to Xero with that day's summary", but the drawing's Money taken goes to lists only Cash, Card, Customer accounts and Gift cards.

**The answer: option 1.** An "Online sales (paid on the website)" row is added to Money taken goes to, so the accountant sees the website's payouts apart.

## 4. Which closed days can be reopened

The question: only the latest closed day, any closed day with later ones left as they are, or any closed day with later ones worked out again.

**The answer: option 1.** Only the latest closed day can be reopened, because later days' expected cash and carried amounts start from what an earlier close banked and left.
