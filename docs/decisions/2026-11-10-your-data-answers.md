# Jack's answers, 10 Nov 2026: Your data › Download everything

Questions from writing the spec for Settings › Office › Your data › Download everything (`openspec/changes/your-data-download`, drawing `set-data-export`), answered by Jack on 10 Nov and passed on by the Front desk session. He chose the recommendation on every one. Each answer names what it settles.

## 1. Where the rows left out of the Citrus Lime import show

The question: "Everything's sorted" (`mv-sorted`) sends See them to Your data, but the Your data drawing doesn't show left-out rows. On 4 Nov See them was hidden until this page exists (`2026-11-04-answers.md`, answer 1).

**The recommendation.** A fifth spreadsheet, "Left out from Citrus Lime", in the same download, with a line in the Download everything fold saying how many rows there are. No new screen. This finishes WP-2.4's See them.

## 2. Whether a shop can choose which lists to download

**The recommendation.** No: all four (Customers, Sales and refunds, Stock, Workshop jobs) every time, as drawn. The ticks only show what is included. (The audit's suggestion of tick boxes, `setup-ui-audit.md` M18, is not taken.)

## 3. Sales: one row per item, or one per sale

**The recommendation.** One row per item sold, with the sale's date, receipt number and total repeated on each row.

## 4. Refunds before the till can refund

**The recommendation.** The refunds Wheelhouse records today (online deposits and Shopify orders) go in now as minus rows. Till refunds are added when Mark's step C records them.

## 5. The daily figures for accounts

The question: the Xero drawing says "Download everything" has the same daily figures (`reports.mjs:345, 360`).

**The recommendation.** Left out until Reports and accounts software are built. Download everything holds the four drawn lists (and answer 1's fifth).

## 6. Staff notes on jobs

**The recommendation.** Included in the shop's own copy. A customer's copy still leaves them out (`2026-10-30-answers.md`, answer 1).

## 7. Recording who downloaded

**The recommendation.** Not recorded until Mark's activity log (WP-1.2) exists. Only the Owner can download meanwhile. No migration.
