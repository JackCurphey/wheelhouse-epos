# Shops and sites — Jack's decisions (5 Oct 2026)

Issue #133 (Codex finding 7): before stage 1, say what belongs to the whole
business and what belongs to each physical shop (a "site"). Multiple sites
decision 2 already settles most of it; the survey found three things Jack
had to decide. Jack answered them one at a time on 5 Oct. The full split is
in `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-wp-1-4-shops-and-sites.md`.

1. **Which settings can differ between shops** (Jack: "1"). Set per shop,
   because they are about the building or how that shop runs day to day:
   storage slots (its own hooks), online booking (exact times or drop-off,
   the drop-off window, notice), when "Close the day" appears, and where
   "Take payment" goes. Shared, because they are the business's rules: the
   blind cash count, what a diary block shows, showing prices online (one
   website) and trust PIN. Address, phone, opening hours and the diary were
   already per shop (Multiple sites 2). Chosen over everything else shared,
   and over moving some of these to the other side.
2. **Repair job numbers run once for the whole business** (Jack: "1").
   WH-1042 at one shop is followed by WH-1043 at the other; a number never
   repeats, so either shop finds a job from its number. As the app works
   today. Chosen over a run per shop with the shop's letter.
3. **One business is one company with one VAT number** (Jack: "1"). Every
   shop's sales go on the same VAT return and "All shops" reports add up. A
   shop run as a separate company is set up as a separate business in
   Wheelhouse, with its own customers and stock. Chosen over each shop
   having its own company and VAT number.
