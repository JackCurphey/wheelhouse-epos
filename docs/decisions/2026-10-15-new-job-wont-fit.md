chosen work is longer than the free time at the chosen slot. The drawing
(`new-job`, `generator/diary.mjs`) ends the warning with a link, "Find the
next free [n] minutes", but nothing said what it does. Jack, 15 Oct, one

   that day or on a following day; the form's time changes and the warning
