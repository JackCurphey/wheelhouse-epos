# Jack's answers, 29 Oct 2026

Questions from the shared workshop computer (WP-1.7 task 3.6,
`openspec/changes/workshop-computer`), answered by Jack in chat on 29 Oct.
Each answer names what it unblocks.

## 1. Set up this computer: "A till" or "A workshop computer"

The question: stage-1 gaps decision 4 says the setup screen offers two
choices, a till or a workshop computer, but only the till was drawn
(`till-setup`). The setup page was built with the choice as pills under
"This computer becomes", the till first and chosen. With "A workshop
computer" chosen, the heading reads "Set up a workshop computer", the line
"This computer becomes a workshop computer: staff work on it with their
PIN.", the shop pills and "Name (optional)" stay, there is no till number,
and the button reads "Make this computer a workshop computer". Jack saw
screenshots at desktop and phone width.

**Approved** (Jack: "Approve it").

What it unblocks: the setup page as built, and setting up the workshop
computer (`till-checkin-workshop`).

## 2. The Owner as a name to tap

The question: the drawings `till-serving-pills` and
`till-checkin-workshop-names` show the Owner as a name to tap, but the
build (#572, #574) never lets the Owner be tapped in: the Owner always
types their PIN, and the Owner's check-in on a workshop computer ends after
10 minutes at most (#571). A tapped-in Owner on a till could give a new
starter a PIN, and tills never time out.

**Keep it** (Jack: "Keep it"), until Owner pages ask for a PIN every time.

## 3. A removed workshop computer

The question: a workshop computer is usually left signed in as the Owner.
Removing it in Settings › Till › Tills (#575) makes that browser count as
staff, not the Owner, until someone signs out there and signs back in.

**Keep it** (Jack: "Keep it").

## 4. The workshop computer's Owner lock and a browser cookie

The question: the Owner lock on a workshop computer (#568) depends on the
`wh_workshop` cookie; someone using the browser's developer tools could
delete it and get the Owner's pages. Closing it needs a server-side change.

**Fix it soon** (Jack: "Fix it soon"): its own piece, built next, with the
full sign-in review.

What it unblocks: the next piece, the Owner lock held on the server.
