Questions from the shared workshop computer (WP-1.7 task 3.6,
`openspec/changes/workshop-computer`), answered by Jack in chat on 29 Oct.
Each answer names what it unblocks.

## 1. Set up this computer: "A till" or "A workshop computer"

The question: stage-1 gaps decision 4 says the setup screen offers two
choices, a till or a workshop computer, but only the till was drawn
(`till-setup`). The setup page was built with the choice as pills under
"This computer becomes", the till first and chosen. With "A workshop
computer" chosen, the heading reads "Set up a workshop computer", the line
"This computer becomes a workshop computer: staff work on it with their
PIN.", the shop pills and "Name (optional)" stay, there is no till number,
and the button reads "Make this computer a workshop computer". Jack saw
screenshots at desktop and phone width.

**Approved** (Jack: "Approve it").

What it unblocks: the setup page as built, and setting up the workshop
computer (`till-checkin-workshop`).

## 2. The Owner as a name to tap

The question: the drawings `till-serving-pills` and
`till-checkin-workshop-names` show the Owner as a name to tap, but the
build (#572, #574) never lets the Owner be tapped in: the Owner always
types their PIN, and the Owner's check-in on a workshop computer ends after
10 minutes at most (#571). A tapped-in Owner on a till could give a new
starter a PIN, and tills never time out.

**Keep it** (Jack: "Keep it"), until Owner pages ask for a PIN every time.

## 5. A day nobody closed

The question: the drawings for a day nobody closed (`op-float-check-unclosed`,
`op-today-unclosed`, `op-close-yesterday`, `op-today-two`) have the morning
float check expect the float plus yesterday's cash, Today say yesterday
wasn't closed with a Close it button, and yesterday closed on its own with
its banking. The 27 Oct answer 9 ("Trust the morning check") instead takes
whatever the morning counts as the start, so yesterday's leftover cash
shows as over at tonight's close and yesterday gets no report of its own.

**Build the drawings** (Jack: "Build the drawings"). This replaces 27 Oct
answer 9 for a day nobody closed only; on a normal day, answer 9 stands.

What it unblocks: a day nobody closed (slice B, WP-3.3 and WP-3.4).

## 7. A normal day with no morning float check

The question: when nobody checks the float in the morning, the Owner looks
at it when closing (27 Oct answer 8, "Looks right" only). Under 27 Oct
answer 9 that look then starts the drawer's figure, so every cash sale made
before it is left out: a £100.00 float and £60.00 of cash sales before a
look at 17:30 expects £100.00, and the day shows £60.00 over. The money
review of a day nobody closed (#580) fixed this only for a day that then
wasn't closed.

**Start from last close** (Jack: "Start from last close"). On a normal day
where nobody does the morning float check, the Owner's look at closing
doesn't start the day's cash. The day starts from the last close, with the
float left in, so all the day's cash sales are expected and the report
comes out right: £100.00 plus £60.00 expected, £60.00 banked, no
difference.

This changes how 27 Oct answers 8 and 9 apply when there is no morning
check: the look at closing is still only a look (answer 8), but it no
longer starts the drawer's figure (answer 9 applies to a morning check).

What it unblocks: the look at closing on a normal day
(`openspec/changes/close-look-normal-day`).
