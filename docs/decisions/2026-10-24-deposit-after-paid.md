# A deposit counted at the till can't be refunded from the job (Jack, 24 Oct 2026)

**Question:** once a job is paid at the till, with its online deposit
counted towards the bill, should staff still be able to refund that deposit
from the job?

**Jack's answer:** "No, not once paid".

**What it means:**
- Paying a job at the till takes its online deposit off what's left to pay;
  the deposit is part of the sale.
- After that, "Refund the deposit" is no longer offered on the job, and the
  server refuses it.
- Any money back then goes through the till's refunds, which aren't built
  yet.
- **This narrows** the 24 Oct morning answer 2, "at any stage"
  (`2026-10-24-morning-questions.md`), to "until the job is paid", by Jack's
  own choice.
