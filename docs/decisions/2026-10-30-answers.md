# Jack's answers, 30 Oct 2026

Questions from building "Download a copy of your data"
(`openspec/changes/your-data-copy`), answered by Jack on 30 Oct. Each answer
names what it unblocks.

## 1. The shop's private notes in a customer's copy of their data

The question: the instant copy a customer downloads from their account
(`ac-download`) holds their details, bikes, repairs, purchases, messages and
what they agreed to, as drawn. It leaves out the shop's own private notes
about the customer and their bikes (the staff-only note on the customer page
and bike), which the account never shows. Under UK data protection a
customer asking the shop for everything is entitled to those notes too; the
shop's Privacy requests list (a later piece) can send a fuller copy by hand.

**Leave notes out** (Jack: "Leave notes out"). The instant copy leaves out
the shop's private notes.

What it unblocks: the copy as built (`decided-while-building.md`, Jack's
lane, 29 Oct, "Download a copy of your data", item 3).

## 2. How often a customer can download a copy of their data

The question came from Jack, reading the limit as built: each sign-in could
take 10 copies in 15 minutes (the review of #587). Jack: "Is there a reason
that we can't stop them from downloading, say, more than once a day? ... 10
times in 15 minutes ... seems like quite a lot." There isn't.

**Once a day** (Jack, 30 Oct, in chat). Each sign-in can download one copy
a day, the shop's day (from midnight in the shop's time zone), counted from
the privacy requests log so a restart never resets it. A second that day is
refused: "You've already downloaded a copy today. You can get another
tomorrow." "Not started? Download it again" saves the same file again
without asking the shop, so a download that didn't save never uses up the
day.

What it unblocks: `openspec/changes/your-data-once-a-day`, replacing the 10
in 15 minutes.

## 3. Deleting a customer's account: what goes and what stays

The question: the drawings (`ac-delete`, `cs-privacy-delete`) and decisions
settle the headline: the customer's details, bikes and messages go; sales
and jobs stay in the shop's books without their name, as the shop must keep
them for tax (customer service decision 9); only a bike still in the
workshop holds a deletion back (coverage walks answers 4 and 5). They don't
settle the rest. Seven defaults were offered.

**Use all seven defaults** (Jack, 30 Oct, in chat: "Use all seven
defaults"):

1. **The customer record stays, blanked:** everything that identifies them
   is cleared and the record is marked deleted, so the sales and jobs that
   point at it are kept.
2. **Their bikes stay as bare rows:** make, model, colour, frame number and
   notes cleared, so a kept job still has a bike, with nothing about it.
3. **Their messages and booking photos go:** the conversation with the shop
   and the photos they sent with a booking. The shop's own staff-only
   workshop notes on a job stay.
4. **The record of messages the shop sent them goes** (each held their
   phone number or email and their name).
5. **Their merges go**, so a merge involving them can't be undone.
6. **The privacy request stays**, with a reference number and no name, as
   the shop's paper trail.
7. **Only the Owner can delete** someone's details, until roles are built.

What it unblocks: "Ask us to delete your account" (`ac-delete`,
`ac-delete-blocked`, `ac-delete-sent`, `ac-account-delete-pending`,
`ac-account-delete-cancelled`) and the shop's Privacy requests list
(`cs-privacy`, `cs-privacy-delete`, `ac-privacy-requests`).

## 4. Their own words on the jobs and orders the shop keeps

The question: answer 3 keeps a deleted customer's jobs and the orders made
with them, for the shop's books. When they booked online, those jobs also
hold what they wrote themselves: the description of the problem, a note
about their bike, their answers to the service's questions, the lines the
booking added to the job's notes, the description in the job's title
("Online booking: [services] - [their words]"), and the same note and title
on the order made with the job. Their words can include a phone number or
other details about them.

**Wipe their words** (Jack, 30 Oct, in chat: "Wipe their words"). On
deletion each of those is replaced with "[removed]". Notes the shop's staff
typed are kept; the work, the prices and the money are untouched.

What it unblocks: deleting someone's details as built (`delete-account`
design 5).
