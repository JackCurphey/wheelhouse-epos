## 1. The shop's private notes in a customer's copy of their data

The question: the instant copy a customer downloads from their account
(`ac-download`) holds their details, bikes, repairs, purchases, messages and
what they agreed to, as drawn. It leaves out the shop's own private notes
about the customer and their bikes (the staff-only note on the customer page
and bike), which the account never shows. Under UK data protection a
customer asking the shop for everything is entitled to those notes too; the
shop's Privacy requests list (a later piece) can send a fuller copy by hand.

**Leave notes out** (Jack: "Leave notes out"). The instant copy leaves out
the shop's private notes.

What it unblocks: the copy as built (`decided-while-building.md`, Jack's
lane, 29 Oct, "Download a copy of your data", item 3).
