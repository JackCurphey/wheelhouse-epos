### 11 Nov 2026: End of day's Close the day and Counting the cash folds, and counting with the expected amount (owner-setup-till-payments-eod, set-eod-close, set-till-quick-saved, set-save-failed, eod-count-shown)

Decided here, for Jack to overrule.

1. **The Counting the cash fold's words.** Checked: `settings-frame.mjs` eodFolds draws only the closed fold, `fold('Counting the cash', 'Count first', open.count || '')`, and no drawing opens it. Owner setup decision 6 says "Blind counting starts on for a new shop … the shop can turn it off in Settings › End of day." The change's settings delta spec already sets the words: one switch, "Count first", with the line "Count the cash before seeing what the till expects.", and the summary "Count first" when on and "Show the expected amount" when off (from cash-up decision 2's "show the expected amount while counting"; design section 5 point 1). What is decided here is only the open fold's layout.
   *Pattern:* a `RowSwitch` row with a line under it, as Signing in's Trust PIN.

2. **The three Close the day choices are radio pills.** Checked: `setup.mjs` closeOpen draws `choice('Show “Close the day” in the till bar', [['At closing time', false], ['30 minutes before', false], ['1 hour before', true]])`, pills with one pressed. Built as one-of-three radio pills, so a screen reader hears it as a choice of one.
   *Pattern:* Signing in's "Go back to the start when left alone" (`RadioPillGroup`).

3. **After Undo, the note says "Saved", with no button; where the note sits.** Checked: `settings-frame.mjs` toastHtml draws the note after a change ("Saved · …" with Undo) and after a failed save (Try again), but nothing after an Undo. An Undo is itself a change that was saved, so the note stays and reads "Saved", with nothing more to undo. Position: toastHtml puts the note `position: absolute` in the settings page's own box (`position: relative; … height: 100%`), which sits inside the main area's padding: `'left: 50%; bottom: 24px; transform: translateX(-50%)'` on a computer and `'left: 12px; right: 12px; bottom: 12px'` on a phone. The app's page scrolls, so the note is fixed to the screen, placed in the same box: the Settings content area inside both the main area's padding (18px at the foot on a computer, 14px on a phone) and Settings' own (about 20px, 16px). On a computer it is 24px above that box's foot and centred over it; on a phone 12px inside it at each side and at the foot. The drawing's main area runs to the foot of the screen, so the foot is the screen's.
   *Pattern:* the same note (`saved-note.tsx`), as drawn.

4. **With Count first off, the difference shows under the counter as the count changes.** Checked: `cashup.mjs` stepCount('shown') draws "Count the drawer and fill in how many of each.", "The till expects [£ expected]", the counter, the total and "Done counting", with no difference line. The difference shows as a "Difference" row under the counter once anything is counted, in the result step's words ("£2.10 short", "£1.00 over").
   *Pattern:* the "Difference" row of the counted step (`eod-count-result`).

5. **While the note shows, space under the page lets the last fold scroll clear of it.** Checked: `settings-frame.mjs` toastHtml places the note `position: absolute` with `'left: 12px; right: 12px; bottom: 12px'` on a phone and `'left: 50%; bottom: 24px; transform: translateX(-50%)'` otherwise, and `setup.mjs` draws `set-till-quick-saved` and `set-save-failed` at desktop, tablet and phone ("Decision 21: every board at desktop, tablet and phone"). Built: the note's look and how long it stays are as drawn; while it shows, the page gets space at its foot as tall as the note and the gap under it, measured, so a two-line failed note on a phone is covered too. On a phone, Counting the cash, the last fold on End of day, was otherwise covered and couldn't be tapped.
   *Pattern:* none built; chosen on review (option 1 of three: space under the page, the note going after a few seconds, or going at the next tap).

6. **Try again only when trying again could work.** Checked: `settings-frame.mjs` line 112, "Decision 17 (H4): the same note after every change; a failed save says so and offers Try again instead of Undo", and `set-save-failed`'s only failure, "Not saved — no internet connection. Built: with no connection, or a fault on the server, the note offers Try again and the change stays on screen; after a refusal (400 or 403), the note shows the server's words with no button, and the fold shows what the server holds.
   *Pattern:* the folds' existing rule, showing the server's own words for a refusal and "Wheelhouse couldn’t save that. Try again in a moment." otherwise.

7. **Float: no Undo for a float that wasn't set; a mistyped float still says so in the fold.** Checked: `setup.mjs:158` draws `set-save-failed` on the Float fold (`eodFolds({ float: floatOpen() })`), and decision 17 puts the note after every change.    *Pattern:* the Close the day and Counting the cash choices (entry 3 and the review fixes).

### 11 Nov 2026: Other ways to pay (owner-setup-till-payments-eod, set-pay-other, set-pay-ways)

Decided here, for Jack to overrule.

1. **Remove is the drawn ✕ button, not a word.** The spec says each row has "an outlined Remove"; the drawing (`setup.mjs` otherRow) draws a borderless 44px button with `icon('close', 16)` and `aria-label="Remove ${esc(t)}"`. Built as drawn: a square 44px ✕ with a 16px icon, named "Remove Finance" and so on. The spec delta now says the same.
   *Pattern:* Storage slots' Remove on each slot (`storage-slots-settings.tsx`), a ✕ in a 44px button named "Remove" and the slot's name; there it is a round chip button with a 14px icon.
2. **The summary names the ways as they are written.** The drawn summary is "Finance, Cycle to Work, payment link" (`settings-frame.mjs` payFolds; the spec's scenario repeats it), with "payment link" in small letters. No rule makes those words from the list (Cycle to Work keeps its capitals), so the summary is the names joined with commas: "Finance, Cycle to Work, Payment link", or "None". The spec delta's scenario now reads the same.
   *Pattern:* Customer groups' summary, the groups' names joined with ", " (`customer-groups-settings.tsx` useCustomerGroupsSummary).
3. **Saved and Undo show in the fold.** The drawing's "Saved · Undo" is a dark note at the foot of the page (`settings-frame.mjs` toastHtml); that shared note isn't built on main yet. Until it is, Add and Remove show "Saved." with an Undo button on a status line under the fold's note, and an error replaces it.
   *Pattern:* the "Saved." status line in Ways to pay (`ways-to-pay-settings.tsx`), and the ghost Undo button in Categories (`categories-settings.tsx`, "Undo removing").
4. **Undo puts a removed way back where it was, with its line.** Design 4 says "A shop's own rows have no line." The Add box sends only a name, so a way the shop types has none; the line is sent only by Undo, to put back a drawn row as it was. A line is up to 120 characters (not drawn; the longest drawn line is 55).
   *Pattern:* quick buttons' Undo, a POST with the position it came from (`server/routes/quick-buttons.js`, on main from 12 Nov: "Last in its group, or at `position` (Undo puts a removed button back there)").
5. **A name already on the list says so.** Not drawn. The server refuses it with "That way to pay is already on the list", and the fold shows those words, keeping what was typed. A name over 30 characters can't be typed (the box stops at 30).
   *Pattern:* a taken name refused with 409 and short words, as a taken till or site code is (`sites-tills.js`: "That site code is taken", with a unique-index clash also answered 409); the server's own words shown in the fold, as Ways to pay shows them (`ways-to-pay-settings.tsx` words()).
6. **Ways to pay's summary starts with Cash.** Cash is built and always on, and the drawn summary is "Cash, card and 4 more" (`settings-frame.mjs` payFolds). With only Cash, store credit and customer accounts built, all of them fit, so it names each that is on in the same words: "Cash", "Cash and store credit", "Cash, store credit and customer accounts". It never reads "None switched on yet" now. "and [n] more" waits until more ways are built.
   *Pattern:* the drawing's own summary; there is no built "and [n] more" summary to follow.
7. **Where the focus goes.** After Remove, the next row's Remove, or the Add box when it was the last; after Undo of a Remove, the restored row's Remove; after Undo of an Add, the Add box (not asked for; the row it would go to is gone).
   *Pattern:* Storage slots' Remove moves the focus to the next chip's Remove (`storage-slots-settings.tsx`); there, with none after it, it goes to the one before, then "+ Add a slot".
