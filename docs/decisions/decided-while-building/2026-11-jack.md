### 11 Nov 2026: Quick buttons on the server (`openspec/changes/owner-setup-till-payments-eod` group 1, `set-till-quick`, `set-till-quick-add`)

Decided here, for Jack to overrule.

1. **A button whose item is off sale stays, marked "Not on sale".** The
   server sends `onSale: false` for it; the till will hide it and the Settings
   row will say "Not on sale". Checked: `setup.mjs` draws no off-sale button,
   and design.md section 5 point 4 says "'Not on sale' on its Settings row,
   hidden on the till". No built screen marks an off-sale item this way (the
   services list hides inactive services), so this is new.
2. **Name limits: a group up to 20 characters, a button's label up to 40.**
   An empty label shows the item's own name. Checked: no limit is drawn, and
   design.md section 5 point 5 sets them. Chosen to fit: a group is a pill,
   and a button is a tile. *Pattern:* the nearest built limit is a booking
   slot's name, 40 characters (`server/routes/workshop-settings.js`).
3. **A button stays when its product or service is deleted, marked "Not on
   sale".** Deleting either in the app only turns it off, so the button
   follows point 1. Only if the row itself is removed (today, when a whole
   shop is removed) do its buttons go with it. Not drawn, and no decision
   covers it. *Pattern:* a product's price history (migration 122) and a
   service's included services (migration 031) go with a removed row the
   same way.

### 12 Nov 2026: Cycle to Work settings and providers (cycle-to-work, cw-settings, cw-settings-provider)

Decided here, for Jack to overrule.

1. **The hold, reminder and quote-valid days start empty.** The drawings
   show them as `[n]`, so no number is invented; the Owner fills them in.
   Until then a quote has no valid-until date and a bike no hold-until date.
   *Pattern:* bracketed gaps stay empty (project rule "never invent data").
2. **Limits: hold 1 to 365 days, reminder 0 to 365, quote valid 1 to 365,
   a provider's name up to 60 characters, "How to apply" and "Checks at
   hand-over" up to 2000; references and certificate numbers up to 60, a
   reason up to 500.** Not drawn. *Pattern:* the nearest built limits: a
   workshop computer's name, 60 (migration 101); a shop message's wording,
   2000 (migration 085).
3. **Commission and deposit are stored as whole numbers: pence for £, and
   hundredths of a percent for %, so 4.5% is 450.** Switching between % and
   £ needs the amount given again, so an old number is never read in the new
   unit. *Pattern:* money is in pence everywhere in the app.
4. **Everyone signed in can read the settings and the providers; only the
   Owner changes them.** A new order needs the hold days and the providers
   to pick from. Someone who can't see costs and margin is never sent a
   provider's commission, but does see its days to pay and hand-over checks.
   *Pattern:* quick buttons (read by all, changed by the Owner) and product
   costs (left out by `canSeeCosts`).
5. **A retired provider is kept, not deleted, and can't be edited.** It
   drops off the list to pick from; Settings can still list it. Orders keep
   theirs. *Pattern:* products and services are turned off, not deleted.
6. **The On Today fold's two `[n]`s ("No certificate after [n] days ·
   payment [n] days late") are two more settings, empty until the Owner
   fills them in, 1 to 365 days.** `cw-settings` draws them as gaps with no
   field of their own. *Pattern:* point 1.

### 12 Nov 2026: The Cycle to Work settings screen (cycle-to-work PR 2, cw-settings, cw-settings-provider)

Decided here, for Jack to overrule.

1. **Each fold's summary is built from what is set, in the drawn words,
   and says "Not set yet" for a gap.** For example, "Held from the quote
   for 5 days · quotes valid for 30 days", with only the parts filled in;
   "No providers yet" with none; On Today says "Off" when its switch is
   off. Checked: `settings-frame.mjs` c2wFolds draws the summaries with
   `[n]` gaps. *Pattern:* End of day's Float ("Not set yet") and Customer
   groups ("No groups yet").
2. **The On Today fold holds a switch, "Show these on Today", and the two
   day boxes, "No certificate after" and "Payment late after".** Checked:
   the fold is drawn closed, with no contents (item 6 above made them
   settings). *Pattern:* the drawn day rows in the same area (c2w.mjs
   numRow) and the settings switch (`row-switch.tsx`).
3. **Picking "Straight away, with a deposit" shows the server's reason, and
   the choice stays where it was.** It stays off until the till can take a
   deposit for an order (`docs/decisions/2026-11-12-cycle-to-work-defaults-taken.md`
   item 1). *Pattern:* every settings box shows the server's own words when
   it says no.
4. **Retire asks first in a box: "Retire [Provider]?", "It comes off the
   list staff pick from for new orders. Orders that already use it keep it.
   A retired provider can't be brought back.", with "Keep [Provider]" and
   "Retire [Provider]".** Checked: `c2w.mjs` draws the Retire link but no
   confirmation, and retiring can't be undone (item 5 above). *Pattern:*
   Tills' Remove box (`tills-settings.tsx`).
5. **"+ Add a provider" opens the drawn Edit provider box, titled "Add a
   provider" and empty, accessories inside what the provider pays.**
   Checked: only Edit is drawn. *Pattern:* Customer groups' Add and Edit
   share one box.
6. **The provider row's line shows only the terms filled in, or "Terms not
   filled in yet".** A commission is left out for someone who can't see
   costs. Checked: `c2w.mjs` draws "Commission [%] · pays in about [n]
   days · [n] checks at hand-over". *Pattern:* point 1.
7. **On a phone, the Front desk line keeps "Cycle to Work" capitalised:
   "Till, payments, messages, end of day, Cycle to Work".** The line used
   to lowercase every title after the first; a title with capitals of its
   own is a name and now keeps them. Not drawn for a phone.
8. **Picking the other unit for the deposit or a commission empties the box
   and asks for the amount in that unit ("Type the deposit as a
   percentage"); nothing is saved until an amount is typed.** An amount
   typed and not yet saved, then the unit picked, is saved once in the unit
   picked. So £50 is never read as 50%. Not drawn. *Pattern:* item 3 of
   the settings section above (the server asks for the amount again).
9. **An Owner editing a provider from a device that isn't sent costs sees no
   Commission box, and saving leaves the commission as it was.** Not drawn.
   *Pattern:* costs are left out by `canSeeCosts` everywhere else.
10. **Small differences from the drawing, each following a built screen:**
    the £ and % switch is two separate pills (`tills-settings.tsx` pills),
    not one joined control; "Pays in about" is a box then the word "days"
    (c2w.mjs numRow); Edit and Retire are ghost buttons with the provider's
    name read out (Customer groups’ row buttons); the provider box is 520
    wide, a width 13 other boxes use, not the drawn 580.
