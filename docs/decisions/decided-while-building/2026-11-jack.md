### 12 Nov 2026: Cycle to Work settings and providers (cycle-to-work, cw-settings, cw-settings-provider)

Decided here, for Jack to overrule.

1. **The hold, reminder and quote-valid days start empty.** The drawings
   show them as `[n]`, so no number is invented; the Owner fills them in.
   Until then a quote has no valid-until date and a bike no hold-until date.
   *Pattern:* bracketed gaps stay empty (project rule "never invent data").
2. **Limits: hold 1 to 365 days, reminder 0 to 365, quote valid 1 to 365,
   a provider's name up to 60 characters, "How to apply" and "Checks at
   hand-over" up to 2000; references and certificate numbers up to 60, a
   reason up to 500.** Not drawn. *Pattern:* the nearest built limits: a
   workshop computer's name, 60 (migration 101); a shop message's wording,
   2000 (migration 085).
3. **Commission and deposit are stored as whole numbers: pence for £, and
   hundredths of a percent for %, so 4.5% is 450.** Switching between % and
   £ needs the amount given again, so an old number is never read in the new
   unit. *Pattern:* money is in pence everywhere in the app.
4. **Everyone signed in can read the settings and the providers; only the
   Owner changes them.** A new order needs the hold days and the providers
   to pick from. Someone who can't see costs and margin is never sent a
   provider's commission, but does see its days to pay and hand-over checks.
   *Pattern:* quick buttons (read by all, changed by the Owner) and product
   costs (left out by `canSeeCosts`).
5. **A retired provider is kept, not deleted, and can't be edited.** It
   drops off the list to pick from; Settings can still list it. Orders keep
   theirs. *Pattern:* products and services are turned off, not deleted.
6. **The On Today fold's two `[n]`s ("No certificate after [n] days ·
   payment [n] days late") are two more settings, empty until the Owner
   fills them in, 1 to 365 days.** `cw-settings` draws them as gaps with no
   field of their own. *Pattern:* point 1.
