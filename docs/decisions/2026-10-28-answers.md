# Jack's answers, 28 Oct 2026

Questions from till PINs and the PIN check-in (WP-1.7,
`openspec/changes/pins-and-checkin`), answered by Jack in chat on 28 Oct.
Each answer names what it unblocks.

## 1. Wrong PINs: slow it down

The question: signing in decision 8 says there is no lock after wrong PINs,
and roles and switches answer 2 relies on that. With no limit at all,
someone could try every one of the 10,000 four-digit PINs in a few minutes.

**Slow it down** (Jack: "Yes, slow it down"). After 10 tries at a shop in
10 minutes, each further try waits 3 seconds before it is checked. There is
no lock-out: the right PIN always works, after its wait. Nobody sees a
"locked" message.

What it unblocks: the PIN check-in as built (`till-checkin`, `till-pin-wrong`).

## 2. The old way to type someone's PIN: remove it

The question: the offline core's `PUT /api/employees/:id/pin` (migration
036) let the Owner type any PIN for someone. Signing in decisions 6 and 7
chose PINs that Wheelhouse picks over PINs a manager sets. No screen used it.

**Remove it** (Jack: "Remove it"). Wheelhouse picks every PIN; nobody types
one for someone else.

What it unblocks: one way to set a PIN, the drawn one.

## 3. Till-only staff: give a PIN at the till now

The question: someone with no email ("till only") can't get a PIN in Your
settings, so until "Give [name] their PIN" exists they can't check in.

**Build giving a PIN at the till now** (Jack: "Build give a PIN now"),
following the drawing (`till-give-pin`).

What it unblocks: till-only staff checking in from day one.

**Not built yet: the drawing isn't complete.** The pop-up itself is drawn
(`signin.mjs` `till-give-pin`: "[Name]’s till PIN", Keep this PIN, Give me a
different one, "Then the till goes back to the PIN screen"). The way in is
only a written line (`consolidate/j11.mjs:95`: a "Serving: [name]" menu for
the Owner or a manager holding "Give [name] their PIN"). Not drawn: that
menu (what else is in it, where Switch goes), how the Owner picks which
person when more than one has no PIN, and adding a till-only person
(`set-staff-invite-till-only`, not built in the new app). Waiting for those
to be drawn.
