# Jack's answers, 4 Nov 2026: Fix and Ask us to help

From the independent review of the rest of the Citrus Lime import (#602 to #605), answered by Jack on 4 Nov and passed on by the session running the build. `docs/decisions/2026-11-03-answers.md` holds the answers before these.

## 1. See them on Everything's sorted

The question: `mv-sorted` links to Settings › Office › Your data, which isn't built, so there's nowhere for See them to go.

Jack chose to hide See them for now and say the rows are kept, and will be listed under Your data. The link comes back when that page is built. He accepted that until then a shop can't see which rows it left out.

## 2. Help requests that mention a customer

The question: someone asking for help may write a customer's details in the box, and those would stay after that customer is deleted.

Jack chose two things. The form gets a line asking people not to include customers' details. When a customer is deleted, any request containing that person's exact email, phone or name is cleared, in any case, with phones matched in their usual forms. He accepted that a misspelt name gets through.

What it unblocks: `openspec/changes/citrus-import-fix`, design 15. The whole request is deleted, as nothing identifying may stay and the request has no use without its text.

## 3. Ask us to help until a real inbox exists

The question: Ask us to help thanks the owner, but until a real inbox exists nobody at Wheelhouse sees what they wrote.

Jack chose to keep the screen as drawn, with a check that stops it working for a real shop. On production with no real inbox connected, the route refuses and the page doesn't offer Ask us to help. The test copy, local development and tests keep the pretend inbox. Anything unknown counts as no pretend inbox.

**A real inbox is needed before the first real shop.** It's a real account, so it's Jack's to say.

## 4. How exactly a help request has to match (4 Nov)

The question, from the re-check of answer 2: matching any word or any run of digits cleared requests that had nothing to do with the customer. A customer called Will cleared "The import will not finish for Sam Patel", and a phone of 555 1234 cleared a request quoting barcode 5012345551234.

Jack chose to tighten it. A name must be at least two words. A phone must be the whole number, not part of a longer one: no digit either side, once spaces and punctuation in the number are allowed for. Email matching stays as it is. He accepted that a customer with a one-word name stays in a request mentioning them, and so does a phone number typed straight after another number.

## 5. Sizes and colours: what sits underneath (WP-1.5)

The question: stock control decision 4 chose one product with a grid on its page. Behind the screens, should each size and colour be a row in `products` joined in a group, or a new table with a new link on every sold, ordered, received and counted line?

Jack chose the first: each size stays its own product behind the scenes, joined in a group. New screens still show one product with a grid. He accepted that the old stock list and the old till's search show each size separately until they're retired.

What it unblocks: piece 5 of `openspec/changes/wp-1-5-products/` (design 3), and the Citrus import linking its per-code products into groups, moving no stock (piece 7).

## 6. Applying a very large count, and sales during it (5 Nov)

The question, from the independent review of `stock-movements-say-who`: applying a count holds its products, and a sale of one of them is told to try again after 2 seconds. At 50,000 products, Apply held them for 19 seconds.

Jack chose to make applying a count faster and keep the 2-second limit. He accepted that during a very large count (50,000 products) a sale could still be told to try again for about 6 seconds.
