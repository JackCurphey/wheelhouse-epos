# Jack's answers, 4 Nov 2026: Fix and Ask us to help

From the independent review of the rest of the Citrus Lime import (#602 to #605), answered by Jack on 4 Nov and passed on by the session running the build. `docs/decisions/2026-11-03-answers.md` holds the answers before these.

## 1. See them on Everything's sorted

The question: `mv-sorted` links to Settings › Office › Your data, which isn't built, so there's nowhere for See them to go.

Jack chose to hide See them for now and say the rows are kept, and will be listed under Your data. The link comes back when that page is built. He accepted that until then a shop can't see which rows it left out.

## 2. Help requests that mention a customer

The question: someone asking for help may write a customer's details in the box, and those would stay after that customer is deleted.

Jack chose two things. The form gets a line asking people not to include customers' details. When a customer is deleted, any request containing that person's exact email, phone or name is cleared, in any case, with phones matched in their usual forms. He accepted that a misspelt name gets through.

What it unblocks: `openspec/changes/citrus-import-fix`, design 15. The whole request is deleted, as nothing identifying may stay and the request has no use without its text.

## 3. Ask us to help until a real inbox exists

The question: Ask us to help thanks the owner, but until a real inbox exists nobody at Wheelhouse sees what they wrote.

Jack chose to keep the screen as drawn, with a check that stops it working for a real shop. On production with no real inbox connected, the route refuses and the page doesn't offer Ask us to help. The test copy, local development and tests keep the pretend inbox. Anything unknown counts as no pretend inbox.

**A real inbox is needed before the first real shop.** It's a real account, so it's Jack's to say.
