# The text receipt and a shop's own messages: Jack's answers (26 Oct 2026)

Asked overnight on 26 Oct, when both pieces reached questions the drawings
and earlier decisions didn't settle. Jack took the suggested answer for each
("Take your suggested answers for both").

## The text receipt (`cp-receipt-text`, `cp-receipt-address-text`)

1. **It waits for Mark's sales-in-pence step C**, which makes the till's
   receipt numbers (B1-[0000]). It doesn't ship first showing "Sale [n]".
2. **No "Download receipt (PDF)" yet.**
3. **No barcode yet** on the receipt page.

## A shop's own automatic messages (`set-msg-new`; owner setup decision 14)

1. **Which yes counts:** a customer's yes to offers and news (marketing
   permission). Job updates still always go.
2. **Stop:** the link in one of these messages stops just that message for
   that customer, not every message the shop has made.
3. **"After a sale"** goes only for a sale with a customer on it.
4. **Removing:** the message's box has Remove, as customer groups do.
5. **The email subject** is the message's name.
