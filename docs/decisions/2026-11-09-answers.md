# Jack's answers, 9 Nov 2026: finishing Customers (WP-3.2)

Questions from writing the spec for the rest of WP-3.2 (`openspec/changes/finish-customers`), answered by Jack on 9 Nov and passed on by the Front desk session. Jack chose the same day to have each piece's spec written one piece ahead, with its questions brought to him before building. Each answer names what it settles.

## 1. The till's half of store credit and accounts

The question: store credit and accounts only become fully useful once the till can put a sale on account, spend and earn store credit, refund onto store credit and take an account payment. Those change how a sale is paid, which is Mark's step C.

**Option 1.** The customer-page side is built now; the till's half is a later, separate piece, after a word with Mark. It is kept out of this change's tasks.

## 2. Who can pay later

The question: the Ways to pay drawing says the limit is "For everyone"; the till's drawing says "Only customers with an account can pay this way".

**Option 1.** Only customers the Owner gives an account ("Give [name] an account") can pay later.

## 3. Where a bike's warranty comes from

The question offered a length per product, per category or per shop. Jack changed it, in his own words: warranties differ by brand, and a bike bought from the shop can carry more than one. Trek is lifetime on the frame and 2 years on components. Cube is 10 years on the frame and 2 years on components. A Trek e-bike is lifetime on the frame and 2 years on the electronics.

**Settled:** warranties are set per brand, as a list of lines (Frame, Components, Electronics), each a number of years or Lifetime. The Electronics line applies only to e-bikes. A product (model) can replace its brand's lines with its own, a small default he can overrule. A bike sold by the shop shows every line with its own end date ("Frame: lifetime · Components: until [date] · Electronics: until [date]"), with months left worked out per line. Warranties only ever show on bikes bought from the shop. Nothing recorded a product's brand, so the change adds one (design § 6).

## 4. Which bikes show a warranty

**Option 1.** Bikes sold through Wheelhouse, plus staff can type "Bought here on" in a bike's Edit for bikes sold before Wheelhouse. With answer 3, the end dates follow from that date and the bike's brand or model lines; staff pick the brand (and model) when the bike isn't linked to a product.

## 5. Money owed before Wheelhouse

**Option 1.** The Owner types "Owed from before Wheelhouse" once per customer, on the Account pop-up.

## 6. Send the copy

**Option 1.** The full copy, with the shop's notes, to download, and emailed when the customer has an email.
