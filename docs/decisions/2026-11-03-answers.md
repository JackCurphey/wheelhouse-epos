# Jack's answers, 3 Nov 2026

From the independent review of the Citrus Lime import (#598 to #600), answered by Jack on 3 Nov, in chat, and passed on by the session running the build. Each answer names what it unblocks.

## 1. What a deleted customer leaves behind

The question: a file sent to bring customers across must not bring back someone who asked for their details to be deleted. Wheelhouse has to remember something to spot them, but Jack's 30 Oct answer 3 clears everything that identifies them.

**"Keep a scrambled code"** (Jack, 3 Nov).

## 2. Which details

**"Yes, scramble all three"** (Jack, 3 Nov). Account number, email and phone are kept only as one-way codes, unique to each shop, used only to spot and skip a deleted person on import.

What it unblocks: `openspec/changes/citrus-import-customers`, design 10. The readable account number no longer stays on a deleted customer's record. This replaces the 3 Nov entry in `decided-while-building.md`.

## 3. When only the email or phone matches (4 Nov)

The question, from the re-review: a deleted customer's email or phone can be someone else's too, such as a son using his mum's email, or two people sharing one placeholder address. Skipping them would silently lose a real customer; bringing them in could bring back the person who was deleted.

**"Needs a look"** (Jack, 4 Nov). A matching account number is always skipped. If only the email or phone matches, the row needs a look, with the reason "Shares contact details with a customer who was deleted", no name and no customer id. It neither comes across nor is dropped silently.

What it unblocks: `openspec/changes/citrus-import-customers`, design 19. Until Fix and Leave it out are built, such a row stays waiting and nothing creates the customer. The summary's "left out, as they asked" counts only account-number matches.

## 4. A waiting row that shares a deleted customer's details (4 Nov)

The question: a son's row is waiting as "the same person as" his mum, because they share an email, and then the mum's details are deleted. Removing the row would lose him from that file without anyone noticing, until the file was sent again. Keeping it keeps the shared email in the row.

**Keep the son's row** (Jack, 4 Nov), accepting that the shared email stays in it until the shop sorts it out.

A row that only matched the deleted customer on email or phone stays as needs a look, with answer 3's reason, "Shares contact details with a customer who was deleted". Nothing in it links to her: no customer id, and not her name. The row's own details from the file stay, including the shared email or phone. A row with her account number is her own, and is still removed.

This replaces the part of the earlier rule for deleting someone's details that removed these rows (the review of 3 Nov; `openspec/changes/citrus-import-customers`, design 8 and 14).

What it unblocks: `openspec/changes/citrus-import-customers`, design 22.
