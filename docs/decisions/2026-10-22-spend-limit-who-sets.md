# Who sets a customer's spending limit (22 Oct 2026)

**The question.** The customer sets a spending limit when booking online
(Workshop day 41, `2026-09-27-workshop-day-review.md`). Nothing was drawn for
staff to set or change one, for example when a customer phones in.

**Options put to Jack.**
1. Only the customer, when booking online; staff can't change it.
2. Staff too, on the job page (to be drawn for approval first).

**Jack's answer** (22 Oct): "1: only the customer sets it, when booking
online; staff can't change it."

**What it means.** A job made by staff, or booked without a limit, always
asks before extra work: staff send a quote. A customer who wants to change
their limit doesn't do it through staff; their quote answers cover the
extra work instead.
