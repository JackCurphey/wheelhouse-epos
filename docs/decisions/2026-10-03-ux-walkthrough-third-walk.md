# The third walk-through — Jack's answers (3 Oct 2026)

Issue #116 step 6 walked the twelve stories again, this time by clicking
through the mockup at desktop, tablet and phone
(`docs/design/user-journeys/walk-3/`). Ten questions came out of it. Jack
answered them on 3 Oct from a checklist, every one with option 1. The full
options and trade-offs are in each walk file's "Questions for Jack".

1. **The search is drawn, not just written as lines** (walk-through 9 Q1 and
   walk-through 10 Q1). The till search's rows are redrawn as decided: the
   name that opens, the till button on the right, and the job's stage. A
   job row reads "Expected 11:30 · Book in" or "Paid online · [date] · Hand
   over" in place of "Add to basket". There is one new drawing: the search
   open on a staff page.
2. **The Reports figures strip shows this week so far**, the same period every
   report opens on ("So far: Mon 14 – Thu 17 September") (walk-through 11
   Q1). This settles the period left open in Reports and accounts' "strip and
   the shop menu" later change.
3. **A shop's name in the strip opens that shop's Sales report**, with the
   shop menu switched to that shop (walk-through 11 Q2). This settles what
   the shop's link opens, also left open there.
4. **Paid with only the Cycle to Work certificate:** the till's paid box reads
   "Paid · Cycle to Work · [Provider]", with nothing from Maya (walk-through
   5 Q1). The two-payment box stays as a situation, reached when something is
   added at the counter.
5. **Story 6 is re-routed in the mockup through the Lightspeed screens**
   (`ls-book-in`, `ls-job-sent`, Mark ready there) (walk-through 6 Q1).
   Other shared pages stay as a normal shop's until Lightspeed is built after
   the trading week (build-plan question 5).
6. **"Show [Second site] to customers" is one press.** The shop shows on the
   website, in booking and for collecting straight away. The checklist step
   ticks, with "Undo" for a moment, like "Hold longer" (walk-through 7 Q1).
7. **The mockup lists each screen's situation lines under the drawing**, as
   a plain read-only list, the same lines as the canvas (walk-through 8 Q1).
8. **Two of story 8's moments are drawn as situations** (walk-through 8 Q2):
   - the workshop computer's "Enter your PIN", with no till number, whose
     keys lead to the diary as that person ("Now working: Alex Morgan");
   - the till search with WH-1042 "Paid online · [date] · Hand over",
     leading to `till-hand-over-job`.
9. **"See it in your account" on the receipt email opens that receipt after
   the sign-in code**, with the account behind it (walk-through 1 Q1). A
   sign-in link carries the page it came from.
10. **The Getting started checklist for a shop moving from Citrus Lime is
    drawn now** (`fr-today-moving`, consolidation-back-office.md finding 5).
    It replaces the practice-mode wording dropped by issue #116 question 4.
